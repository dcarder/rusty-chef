How The Rusty Chef works

  1. Tell it what you have. Tick off the ingredients in your kitchen in the Pantry Tool.
  2. Get matching recipes. We show which of our recipes you can make right now, and what you'd be missing for the rest.
  3. Cook, save and shop. Save your favorites, use the built-in step timers while you cook, and send missing ingredients straight to your Walmart cart.

Every recipe on the site is written to work with common pantry staples, and each one includes swaps for ingredients you might not have. Our robot mascot can't taste a thing, so it relies on solid technique, reliable ratios and well-tested flavor pairings. It turns out that's a pretty good approach for home cooks too.
