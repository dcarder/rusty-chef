Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Rusty Chef ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit therustychef.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

  • Information you enter: pantry selections and saved recipes are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to us, and you can delete them at any time by clearing your browser data.
  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and message.
  • Shop orders: if you order from our Shop, we collect your name, email, phone number (optional), shipping address and Cash App name to process and ship your order. The order form is delivered to our email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Payments are made in Cash App; we never see your card or bank details. We use this information only to fill your order.
  • Automatically collected information: like most websites, we and our partners may collect your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages and the date and time of your visit.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to keep the Site working, remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the Site and show advertising. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them may affect some features.

Your cookie choices

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before using cookies for personalized ads or analytics. Until you agree, those cookies stay off. Depending on where you live in the United States, you may also see a message that lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising: Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this Site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn more, see how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. See Your cookie choices.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are visited and how long people stay. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site
  • To respond to your messages
  • To understand traffic and usage trends
  • To display advertising that keeps the Site free
  • To detect and prevent abuse or technical problems

Sharing information

We don't sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site (such as hosting, analytics and advertising partners), or when required by law.

Third-party links

The Site may link to other websites. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date above.

Contact us
