About The Rusty Chef

The Rusty Chef started with a simple, slightly embarrassing question: what can I actually make with what's already in my kitchen?

Most recipe sites assume you've just come back from the store with a cart full of fresh herbs and specialty ingredients. Real life looks more like half an onion, a can of chickpeas, some rice and a jar of something you bought for one recipe two years ago. The Rusty Chef is built for that kitchen.

Use the Pantry Tool to tick off what you have and see which recipes you can make tonight, or keep track of your groceries in My Kitchen so you use them up before they go bad. Every recipe on the site is written around common pantry staples and includes swaps for ingredients you might be missing.

Why "No taste buds required"?

Our mascot is a slightly rusty robot chef who can't taste a thing, so it has to rely on solid technique, good ratios and well-tested flavor pairings. It turns out that's a pretty good recipe for home cooking too.

What you'll find here

  • Recipes: 112 breakfasts, soups, dinners, breads and desserts built from pantry basics, each with swaps for ingredients you might be missing.
  • My Kitchen: add the groceries you buy and when they go bad, and Rusty tells you what to cook first so nothing gets wasted. Just back from the store? Snap a photo of your receipt and your groceries are added for you. The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Pantry Tool: tick what you have and see which recipes you can make tonight. Filter for meals that take 20 minutes or less, vegetarian dishes, kid-friendly picks, or meals that cost $1 or less a serving.
  • Skip the Restaurant: your takeout and restaurant favorites made at home for a fraction of the price.
  • Saved Recipes: keep your favorites in one place.
  • Shop: the Rusty Chef phone holder, 3D printed by hand, to keep your phone up and out of the mess while you cook.
  • The Rusty Chef game: a quick break while the water boils.

Built for cooking, not just reading

Every recipe page comes with tools to help you once you're actually in the kitchen:

  • Cook mode: a big-text, one-step-at-a-time view that starts with an ingredient checklist and keeps your screen from going dark while you cook.
  • Servings scaler: cooking for two or feeding a crowd? Change the servings and every ingredient amount adjusts for you.
  • Cost per serving: a rough estimate of what each recipe costs to make, and the total for however many servings you choose.
  • Step timers: tap any time in the instructions to start a timer.
  • Walmart ordering: missing something? Send the ingredients you don't have straight to your Walmart cart.
  • Save and share: save recipes for later or send them to friends and family.

Who's behind it

The Rusty Chef is run by Daniel Carder, a husband, dad and food lover in Ocala, Florida, who cooks for his wife and kids every day. I got tired of the idea that you have to spend a fortune going out to eat to get the kind of food you could easily make at home. Most of the dishes people pay restaurant prices for come down to a few pantry staples and the right technique.

That's what this site is for: real, satisfying meals made from what you already have, with clear steps and swaps when you're missing something. Save the money, skip the reservation, and cook something great tonight.
