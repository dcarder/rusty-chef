Spinach Pesto Pasta

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 22 minutes

Jump to recipe

Classic pesto needs lots of fresh basil, which is pricey and goes bad fast. Spinach makes a cheaper pesto that's just as green and tasty.

Blend it while the pasta boils and lunch is ready in about 20 minutes.

Save the pasta water

A splash of the starchy pasta cooking water helps the pesto turn silky and cling to the pasta instead of clumping.

Spinach Pesto Pasta

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook12 min
  • Total22 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.10/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pasta, such as penne or spaghetti
  • 3 cups spinach
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup parsley or basil (optional)
  • Halved cherry tomatoes, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water until just tender. Save 1/2 cup of the cooking water, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile, blend the spinach, parmesan, garlic, lemon juice, salt and parsley in a blender or food processor.
  3. With the blender running, pour in the olive oil and blend until smooth.
  4. Toss the hot pasta with the pesto, adding splashes of pasta water until it's glossy and coats every piece.
  5. Serve topped with extra parmesan and tomatoes if you have them.

Pantry swaps

  • No parmesan? Use any hard cheese, or 2 tablespoons of nuts or seeds for a dairy-free pesto.
  • No blender? Chop everything as finely as you can and stir it into the oil.

Rusty Chef tip: Pesto darkens when it sits. Press plastic wrap right on the surface of leftover pesto to keep it bright green.

Storage

The pasta keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Extra pesto keeps for a week, or freeze it in ice cube trays.
