Easy Drop Biscuits

By Daniel Carder · Bread · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Drop biscuits are the easiest homemade bread there is. Mix, scoop and bake.

They're perfect with soup, chili, or butter and jam.

From Daniel's kitchen

My grandmother's biscuits took practice, but these are foolproof. No rolling or cutting, just mix and scoop. I make them with soup or with sausage gravy on Sunday mornings, and the kids eat them warm with butter and honey.

Keep the butter cold

Cold butter melts in the oven and creates steam pockets, which is what makes biscuits flaky and tall.

Easy Drop Biscuits

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves8

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 cup cold butter, cubed
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C) and line a baking sheet.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar.
  3. Rub in the cold butter with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces.
  4. Stir in the milk until just combined.
  5. Drop 8 large scoops onto the sheet and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until golden.

Pantry swaps

  • No milk? Use plain yogurt thinned with water.
  • Want cheesy biscuits? Fold in 1 cup shredded cheddar.

Rusty Chef tip: Grate frozen butter on a box grater for the easiest, flakiest biscuits.

Storage

Best the same day. Freeze and reheat for up to 2 months.
