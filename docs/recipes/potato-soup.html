Creamy Potato Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

Potatoes are cheap, last for weeks and turn into one of the most comforting soups imaginable.

This one is creamy without much cream, because the potatoes thicken it naturally. Load it up like a baked potato with cheese and green onions.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

A bag of potatoes goes a long way, and this soup proves it. I make it when money's tight and the weather finally cools down a little here in Florida. Load it up like a baked potato with cheese and green onions, and my wife goes back for seconds while even my pickiest kid cleans his bowl.– Daniel

Mash, don't over-blend

Potatoes get gluey if you blend them too hard. A potato masher or a few short pulses with a stick blender keeps the texture creamy.

Creamy Potato Soup

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.39/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 lbs potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 4 cups stock
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper
  • Shredded cheese (optional)
  • Green onions (optional)

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter and cook the onion for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the potatoes and stock. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until the potatoes are very tender.
  3. Mash until mostly smooth, leaving some chunks.
  4. Stir in the milk and heat through without boiling.
  5. Season generously and top with cheese and green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No stock? Water with extra salt and a bay leaf.
  • No milk? Leave it out and use a little more stock.

Rusty Chef tip: Starchy russet potatoes make the creamiest soup. Waxy potatoes hold their shape for a chunkier result.

Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge. Reheat gently and add a splash of milk.
