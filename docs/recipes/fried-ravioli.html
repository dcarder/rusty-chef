Mika's Fried Ravioli

By Daniel Carder · Snack · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Fried ravioli (also called toasted ravioli) is a St. Louis favorite: crispy breadcrumbs on the outside, cheesy and soft inside.

A bag of frozen ravioli and some breadcrumbs make a party snack or a fun dinner for a fraction of restaurant prices.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This one is named after my daughter Mika, because she is completely obsessed with it. She asks for fried ravioli constantly: after school, on weekends, for her birthday, pretty much any time I ask what she wants for dinner. It starts with the same cheap bag of frozen ravioli I use for baked ravioli, and watching her face when a plate of these hits the table never gets old.– Daniel

Fry from frozen

Don't thaw the ravioli. Frozen ravioli hold their shape while you bread them, and they cook through in the hot oil in about 2 minutes. Thawed ones go soft and can burst.

Mika's Fried Ravioli

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.96/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 bag (25 oz) frozen cheese ravioli
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1 1/2 cups breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • Oil for frying (about 2 inches in the pan)
  • 1 cup marinara or crushed tomatoes, warmed, for dipping

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs and milk in a shallow bowl. Mix the breadcrumbs, herbs and half the parmesan in another bowl.
  2. Dip each frozen ravioli in the egg, then press it into the breadcrumbs to coat both sides. Lay on a plate.
  3. Heat about 2 inches of oil in a deep pan to 350°F (175°C). A breadcrumb dropped in should sizzle right away.
  4. Fry a few at a time for 1 to 2 minutes per side until deep golden. Drain on paper towels.
  5. Sprinkle with the remaining parmesan and serve hot with warm marinara.

Pantry swaps

  • Air fryer? Spray the breaded ravioli with oil and air fry at 400°F for 8 minutes, flipping halfway.
  • Oven-baked? Spray with oil and bake at 425°F for 12 to 15 minutes, flipping once.
  • No breadcrumbs? Crush crackers or cornflakes instead.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't crowd the pan. Adding too many at once cools the oil and makes them greasy instead of crispy.

Storage

Best fresh. Reheat leftovers in the oven or air fryer at 400°F for 5 minutes to crisp them back up.
