Sticky Honey Garlic Chicken

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Honey, soy sauce and garlic make one of the best three-ingredient sauces out there.

Cooked down with the chicken, it turns into a sticky glaze that's great over rice.

Brown first, then glaze

Cook the chicken until golden before adding the sauce. Honey burns easily, so it only goes in once the chicken is almost done.

Sticky Honey Garlic Chicken

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs or breasts
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Cooked rice, to serve
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken dry, season with salt and pepper and toss with the cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  3. Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the honey, soy sauce and vinegar, if using. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes, turning the chicken, until the sauce is thick and sticky.
  5. Serve over rice, spooning the glaze on top, and sprinkle with green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No honey? Use maple syrup or brown sugar.
  • No cornstarch? Skip it. The chicken won't be quite as crisp, but the sauce will still thicken.

Rusty Chef tip: If the sauce gets too thick, add a splash of water. If it's too thin, simmer it a minute longer.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge.
