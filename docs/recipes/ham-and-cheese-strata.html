Ham and Cheese Breakfast Strata

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 60 minutes

Jump to recipe

A strata is a savory bread pudding: cubes of bread soaked in eggs and milk, layered with ham and cheese, then baked until puffed and golden.

Put it together the night before and all you have to do in the morning is turn on the oven.

Let it soak

The bread needs at least an hour, and ideally a night in the fridge, to soak up the custard. That's what gives a strata its soft middle and crisp top.

Ham and Cheese Breakfast Strata

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook45 min
  • Total60 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.74/serving

Ingredients

  • 6 cups cubed bread, preferably a day old
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 cups diced ham
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheese
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon butter, for the dish
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Butter a 9x13-inch baking dish. Spread half the bread in the dish, then scatter over half the ham and half the cheese.
  2. Repeat with the rest of the bread and ham, keeping back the remaining cheese.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, mustard and salt together and pour evenly over the bread. Press down gently so all the bread is soaked.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight.
  5. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Sprinkle with the rest of the cheese and bake uncovered for 40 to 45 minutes, until puffed, golden and set in the middle. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

Pantry swaps

  • No ham? Cooked sausage, bacon or sautéed vegetables.
  • No mustard? Leave it out, or add a pinch of paprika.

Rusty Chef tip: If the top is browning before the middle is set, cover the dish loosely with foil for the last 10 minutes.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Reheat slices in the microwave or a low oven.
