Crispy Baked Honey BBQ Wings

By Daniel Carder · Snack · 60 minutes · Bar Food

Jump to recipe

Wing restaurants deep-fry their wings, but a hot oven and one pantry trick get the skin just as crispy.

The honey BBQ sauce is made from ketchup and honey, so you don't need a bottle of barbecue sauce.

The baking powder trick

Tossing the wings in a little baking powder (not baking soda) before baking dries out the skin so it blisters and crisps in the oven, just like fried wings.

Crispy Baked Honey BBQ Wings

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook50 min
  • Total60 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$4.00/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken wings, split into flats and drumettes
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter (optional)
  • Pinch of chili flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with foil and set a wire rack on top, if you have one.
  2. Pat the wings very dry with paper towels. Toss with the baking powder and salt.
  3. Arrange in a single layer on the rack. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, flipping halfway, until deep golden and crispy.
  4. Meanwhile, simmer the ketchup, honey, vinegar, paprika, garlic powder, butter and chili flakes in a small saucepan for 5 minutes until slightly thickened.
  5. Toss the hot wings in the sauce and serve right away.

Pantry swaps

  • Want buffalo wings? Toss them in 1/3 cup hot sauce melted with 3 tablespoons butter instead.
  • No wire rack? Bake directly on the foil and flip twice so both sides crisp.

Rusty Chef tip: Dry wings are crispy wings. Pat them as dry as you can, or leave them uncovered in the fridge for a few hours first.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes so they crisp back up.
