Simple Red Lentil Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Red lentils cook quickly and break down into a naturally creamy soup, so there's no need for a blender.

It's cheap, filling, high in protein and makes great leftovers.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

When I'm feeling run down or overwhelmed, this is my comfort food. A bag of red lentils costs next to nothing and makes a big pot that lasts for days. It's one of those meals that makes you feel taken care of, even on a budget.– Daniel

Rinse the lentils

Rinsing removes dust and excess starch so the soup isn't foamy or gluey. Swirl them in a sieve under cold water until it runs mostly clear.

Simple Red Lentil Soup

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.14/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced (optional)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 4 cups stock
  • Salt
  • Lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and cook the onion and carrot for 6 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic and cumin for 1 minute.
  3. Add the lentils and stock. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 20 minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Whisk vigorously to break down the lentils, or blend for a smoother soup.
  5. Season with salt and finish with a good squeeze of lemon.

Pantry swaps

  • No carrot? Leave it out or add a diced potato.
  • Brown lentils only? Simmer 35 to 40 minutes and expect a chunkier soup.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't skip the lemon. It's what makes lentil soup taste bright instead of heavy.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in the fridge. It thickens as it sits, so add water when reheating.
