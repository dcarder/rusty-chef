Skillet Chocolate Chip Cookie

By Daniel Carder · Dessert · 32 minutes · American

Jump to recipe

A giant chocolate chip cookie baked in a skillet, gooey in the middle and crispy at the edges, is the dessert everyone fights over at restaurants.

It's one bowl, no mixer and no scooping a dozen little cookies.

Pull it while the middle is soft

A cast-iron skillet holds heat and keeps cooking the cookie after it comes out of the oven. Take it out when the edges are golden and the center still looks a little soft. If it looks fully done in the oven, it'll be dry by the time you serve it.

Skillet Chocolate Chip Cookie

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook22 min
  • Total32 min
  • Serves8
  • Est. cost$0.55/serving

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips, divided
  • Vanilla ice cream, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Melt the butter in a 10-inch cast-iron or other oven-safe skillet over low heat. Turn off the heat and pour the butter into a mixing bowl, leaving a thin coating in the skillet. Let the butter cool for 5 minutes.
  3. Whisk the brown sugar and sugar into the butter, then whisk in the egg and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt just until there's no dry flour left. Fold in 3/4 cup of the chocolate chips.
  5. Press the dough evenly into the skillet and scatter the rest of the chocolate chips on top.
  6. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until the edges are golden brown and the center looks just set but still soft.
  7. Let it cool for 10 minutes, then cut into wedges and serve warm with a scoop of ice cream.

Pantry swaps

  • No cast-iron skillet? Use a greased 9-inch cake pan or pie plate and start checking at 18 minutes.
  • No brown sugar? Use all white sugar. The cookie will be a little crispier and less chewy.
  • Want to mix it up? Swap half the chocolate chips for M&Ms, chopped nuts or peanut butter chips.

Rusty Chef tip: Sprinkle a pinch of flaky or coarse salt over the top as soon as it comes out of the oven. It makes the chocolate taste even richer.

Storage

Cover the skillet and keep it at room temperature for up to 2 days. Warm slices in the microwave for about 15 seconds.
