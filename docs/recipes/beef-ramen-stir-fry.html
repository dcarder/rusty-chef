Beef Ramen Stir-Fry

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 20 minutes · Japanese & Asian

Jump to recipe

Instant ramen noodles are great for stir-fry, not just soup.

With a pound of ground beef and a bag of frozen vegetables, three packs feed a family of four.

Undercook the noodles

Pull the noodles out of the water a minute early. They finish cooking in the sauce and stay springy instead of mushy.

Beef Ramen Stir-Fry

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.60/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 packs instant ramen (seasoning packets not needed)
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups frozen stir-fry or mixed vegetables
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar or honey
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles in boiling water for 2 minutes, just until they loosen. Drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. Stir together the soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger, sesame oil, chili flakes and 1/4 cup water.
  3. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes, breaking it up. Drain most of the fat.
  4. Add the frozen vegetables and garlic and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until hot.
  5. Add the noodles and sauce and toss for 2 minutes until glossy and coated. Top with green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No ground beef? Use ground chicken, ground pork or sliced chicken breast.
  • Meat-free? Scramble 3 eggs in the pan instead of the beef.

Rusty Chef tip: Rinsing the noodles in cold water washes off extra starch so they don't clump in the pan.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet with a splash of water.
