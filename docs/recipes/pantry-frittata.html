Clean-Out-the-Fridge Frittata

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

A frittata is the best way to use up odds and ends: a bit of cheese, leftover vegetables, cooked potatoes or pasta.

It starts on the stove and finishes in the oven, so there's no tricky flipping.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This is what happens to the bits and pieces left at the end of the week. A little cheese, some leftover veggies, a few eggs, and suddenly there's a real dinner. When it's just my wife and me, the leftovers make a great lunch the next day.– Daniel

Finish under the broiler

Cook the bottom on the stove until mostly set, then slide the pan under the broiler for 2 to 3 minutes to puff and brown the top.

Clean-Out-the-Fridge Frittata

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.10/serving

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup cooked vegetables or frozen veg, thawed
  • 1 tablespoon oil or butter
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the broiler. Whisk the eggs, milk, half the cheese, salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in an oven-safe skillet and warm the vegetables for 2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the eggs. Cook on medium-low without stirring for 6 to 8 minutes until the edges are set.
  4. Top with the remaining cheese and broil for 2 to 3 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Rest for 2 minutes, then slice into wedges.

Pantry swaps

  • No oven-safe pan? Cover and cook on low until set.
  • No milk? Leave it out.

Rusty Chef tip: Leftover pasta is fantastic in a frittata. Toss it in with the vegetables.

Storage

Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Good cold in a lunchbox.
