Garlic Butter Noodles

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 14 minutes

Jump to recipe

Sometimes simple is best. Noodles tossed in garlic butter and parmesan is comfort food that comes together in the time it takes to boil water.

Serve as a quick dinner or as a side with roasted vegetables.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Buttery noodles are one of the few things all of my picky kids agree on. I add some garlic and parmesan for the grown-ups. It's the dinner I make when I'm exhausted and need everyone fed without a fight.– Daniel

Brown the butter slightly

Let the butter foam and turn light golden before adding the garlic. Browned butter tastes nutty and rich.

Garlic Butter Noodles

  • Prep2 min
  • Cook12 min
  • Total14 min
  • Serves2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • Salt and pepper
  • Chopped parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta. Save 1/2 cup water before draining.
  2. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat until foamy and lightly golden.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the pasta, parmesan and a splash of pasta water. Toss until creamy.
  5. Season with pepper and parsley.

Pantry swaps

  • No parmesan? Any hard cheese, or skip it.
  • No fresh garlic? 1 teaspoon garlic powder.

Rusty Chef tip: Add a pinch of chili flakes and a squeeze of lemon to keep it from feeling too heavy.

Storage

Best fresh.
