Irish Beef Stew

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 140 minutes · Irish

Jump to recipe

Irish stew is pure comfort: chunks of beef, potatoes and carrots cooked slowly until everything is fork-tender.

Most of the cooking time is hands-off, so you can let it bubble away on a lazy Sunday afternoon.

Brown the beef well

Take your time searing the beef in batches until it's deeply browned. Those brown bits stuck to the bottom of the pot are where the rich gravy flavor comes from.

Irish Beef Stew

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook120 min
  • Total140 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$2.75/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs beef stew meat (chuck), cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 3 tablespoons oil, divided
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 can (14.9 oz) Guinness or other stout (optional)
  • 4 cups beef stock
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 1/2 lbs potatoes, cut into large chunks
  • 3 carrots, cut into large chunks
  • Chopped parsley, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Pat the beef dry and toss it with the salt, a few grinds of pepper and the flour.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in batches, about 5 minutes per batch, then move it to a plate.
  3. Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining oil and the onion and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the Guinness, if using, and scrape up the brown bits from the bottom of the pot. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the stock, thyme and beef. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 1 1/2 hours.
  6. Add the potatoes and carrots and simmer uncovered for 30 to 40 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the gravy has thickened. Season with salt and pepper and top with parsley.

Pantry swaps

  • No Guinness? Use 1 1/2 more cups of beef stock plus 1 tablespoon soy sauce for depth.
  • Have a slow cooker? After browning the beef and onions, put everything in the slow cooker and cook on low for 8 hours.

Rusty Chef tip: Cut the potatoes and carrots into big chunks so they stay whole instead of turning to mush during the long simmer.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer. It tastes even better the next day.
