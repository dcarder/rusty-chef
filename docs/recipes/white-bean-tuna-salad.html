Italian White Bean and Tuna Salad

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

This classic Italian combination turns two cans into a fresh, filling lunch in about 10 minutes.

Eat it on its own, piled on toast or stuffed into a wrap.

Let it sit

Give the salad 10 minutes after dressing it. The beans soak up the vinegar and olive oil and the onion mellows out.

Italian White Bean and Tuna Salad

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.57/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) white beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (5 oz) tuna, drained
  • 1/4 small onion, very thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons vinegar or lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley (optional)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Pinch of chili flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, a pinch of salt and some pepper in a bowl.
  2. Add the onion and let it sit in the dressing for 5 minutes to soften its bite.
  3. Add the beans and tuna, breaking the tuna into large flakes.
  4. Fold in the parsley and chili flakes, if using. Taste and add more salt or vinegar as needed.
  5. Serve straight away or chill for up to a day.

Pantry swaps

  • No white beans? Chickpeas work just as well.
  • No fresh parsley? Use 1/2 teaspoon dried Italian herbs.

Rusty Chef tip: Tuna packed in olive oil makes this even better. Use some of the oil from the can in the dressing.

Storage

Keeps for 2 days in the fridge.
