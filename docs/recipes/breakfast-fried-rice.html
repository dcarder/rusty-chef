Breakfast Fried Rice

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 15 minutes

Jump to recipe

Fried rice for breakfast is common across much of Asia, and it's one of the best uses for last night's leftover rice.

Crispy rice, soft scrambled eggs and salty ham come together in one pan in about 15 minutes.

Why cold rice matters

Freshly cooked rice is too wet and turns mushy in the pan. Rice that has spent a night in the fridge has dried out enough to fry into separate, slightly crispy grains.

Breakfast Fried Rice

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.50/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, preferably a day old
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup diced ham or cooked sausage
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (optional)
  • 1/2 cup frozen vegetables (optional)
  • Hot sauce, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ham and cook for 2 minutes until lightly browned.
  2. Add the garlic and frozen vegetables, if using, and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Push everything to one side, pour in the eggs and scramble until just set.
  4. Add the rice, breaking up any clumps, and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until hot and starting to crisp.
  5. Stir in the soy sauce and most of the green onions. Serve topped with the rest of the onions and hot sauce if you like.

Pantry swaps

  • No ham? Bacon, sausage, leftover chicken or just extra eggs.
  • No soy sauce? Season with salt and a squeeze of lime.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't stir the rice constantly. Let it sit for 30 seconds at a time so the bottom gets crispy.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a hot pan rather than the microwave to bring back the crisp.
