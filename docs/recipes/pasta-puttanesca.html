Pasta Puttanesca

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

Puttanesca is a punchy Neapolitan pasta built entirely from jars and cans: tomatoes, olives, capers and garlic.

It's full of flavor and ready in 20 minutes.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This is a grown-up pasta I make when it's just my wife and me. It's bold, salty and full of flavor, and it comes completely from jars and cans. Twenty minutes, and it feels like a restaurant dinner without the restaurant bill.– Daniel

Go easy on the salt

Olives and capers are already salty. Taste the sauce before adding any extra salt.

Pasta Puttanesca

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.74/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup olives, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons capers
  • Chopped parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water.
  2. Warm the oil, garlic and chili flakes for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes, olives and capers. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Toss the drained pasta in the sauce with a splash of pasta water.
  5. Top with parsley and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No capers? More olives.
  • Like anchovies? Melt 2 or 3 in the oil with the garlic.

Rusty Chef tip: Kalamata olives give the best flavor, but any olives work.

Storage

Sauce keeps 5 days in the fridge.
