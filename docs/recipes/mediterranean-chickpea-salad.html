Mediterranean Chickpea Salad

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 10 minutes · Mediterranean & Middle Eastern

Jump to recipe

This salad leans on pantry staples like chickpeas and olives, so you can make it even when the fridge is nearly empty.

It actually gets better after a few hours, which makes it perfect for packing ahead.

Make it your own

Chickpeas and dressing are the base. After that, add whatever you have: chopped tomato, cucumber, bell pepper, crumbled feta or leftover rice.

Mediterranean Chickpea Salad

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$1.00/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/3 cup olives, sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons vinegar or lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley (optional)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, dried herbs, a pinch of salt and some pepper in a large bowl.
  2. Add the onion and let it sit for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the chickpeas, olives and bell pepper, if using, and toss well.
  4. Stir in the parsley. Taste and add more salt or vinegar if needed.
  5. Serve right away or chill for up to 3 days.

Pantry swaps

  • No olives? Use capers, or leave them out and add a little extra salt.
  • No chickpeas? White beans or cooked lentils.

Rusty Chef tip: Gently crush a few chickpeas with a fork. They thicken the dressing and help it cling.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge.
