Simple Rice and Beans

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Rice and beans is eaten around the world in countless variations because it's cheap, filling and nutritionally complete.

This simple version gets big flavor from onion, garlic and spices.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Rice and beans have fed my wife, my kids and me through some lean times, and I'm not ashamed of it. When it's seasoned right, it's honestly delicious. Top it with a fried egg or some cheese, and it's a full meal for very little money.– Daniel

Season the beans boldly

Beans soak up a lot of seasoning. Taste and add more salt, spice or a splash of vinegar until they're properly savory.

Simple Rice and Beans

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$0.56/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 can (15 oz) kidney or black beans
  • Salt
  • Splash of vinegar or lime (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to the package.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the onion in oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
  3. Add the beans with half their liquid. Simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
  4. Season with salt and a splash of vinegar.
  5. Serve the beans over the rice.

Pantry swaps

  • No paprika? Chili powder.
  • No kidney beans? Any canned bean.

Rusty Chef tip: Top with a fried egg for an even more filling meal.

Storage

Keeps 4 days. Store rice and beans separately.
