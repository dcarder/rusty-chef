Red Lentil Coconut Dal

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Dal is one of the cheapest, most comforting dinners you can make from a pantry.

Red lentils need no soaking and break down into a creamy stew in about 20 minutes.

Which lentils to use

Red or yellow split lentils melt into a creamy dal. Brown or green lentils hold their shape, so they work too, but need about 15 more minutes and a little more water.

Red Lentil Coconut Dal

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.21/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger (optional)
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 2 cups water or stock (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 handfuls spinach (optional)
  • Cooked rice, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, curry powder and ginger and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk, water and salt. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat.
  4. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring often, until the lentils are soft and creamy. Add a splash more water if it gets too thick.
  5. Stir in the spinach until wilted, if using. Taste for salt and serve over rice.

Pantry swaps

  • No coconut milk? Use an extra cup of water and stir in a spoonful of butter or yogurt at the end.
  • No curry powder? Use 1 teaspoon cumin plus 1/2 teaspoon each paprika and ginger.

Rusty Chef tip: A squeeze of lime or a splash of vinegar at the end brightens up all the spices.

Storage

Keeps for 5 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer. It thickens as it sits, so add water when reheating.
