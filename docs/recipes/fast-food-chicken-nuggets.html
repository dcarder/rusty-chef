Fast-Food Style Chicken Nuggets

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Homemade nuggets are made from whole pieces of chicken breast, and they taste fresher than the drive-thru.

They're pan-fried in a little oil, and a batch makes enough for the whole family for less than one fast-food meal.

Season the coating

Fast-food nuggets taste the way they do because the coating is well seasoned. A little paprika, garlic powder and salt in the breadcrumbs makes a big difference.

Fast-Food Style Chicken Nuggets

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.88/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup oil, for frying
  • Ketchup or honey mustard, for dipping (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix the breadcrumbs, flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt in a shallow bowl. Beat the eggs in a second bowl.
  2. Dip each piece of chicken in the egg, then press it into the breadcrumb mix until coated.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat until it shimmers.
  4. Fry the nuggets in batches for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through (165°F inside).
  5. Drain on a paper towel and serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.

Pantry swaps

  • Air fryer? Spray the coated nuggets with oil and air fry at 400°F for 10 to 12 minutes, turning halfway.
  • No breadcrumbs? Use crushed crackers or cornflakes.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't crowd the pan. Leave space between the nuggets so they fry and get crispy instead of steaming.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Freeze cooked nuggets for up to 2 months and reheat in the oven or air fryer.
