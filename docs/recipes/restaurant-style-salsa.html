Mexican Restaurant-Style Salsa

By Daniel Carder · Snack · 5 minutes

Jump to recipe

That addictive salsa at Mexican restaurants is mostly canned tomatoes, onion, garlic and lime.

Five minutes in a blender makes a big jar for less than a small one at the store.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

We go through salsa like water in this house, and the good restaurant-style jars aren't cheap. This blender version tastes just like the salsa they bring out with chips at our favorite Mexican place. I make a big batch on Sunday and it's gone by Friday.– Daniel

Pulse, don't purée

Pulse the blender in short bursts so the salsa stays slightly chunky. Running it nonstop makes it watery and foamy.

Mexican Restaurant-Style Salsa

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total5 min
  • Serves8

Ingredients

  • 1 can (28 oz) whole tomatoes, drained
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 jalapeño or a pinch of chili flakes (optional)
  • Handful of cilantro (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add the onion, garlic and jalapeño to the blender and pulse until finely chopped.
  2. Add the tomatoes, lime juice, cumin, salt and cilantro.
  3. Pulse 8 to 10 times until it's the texture you like.
  4. Taste and add more salt or lime.
  5. Chill for 30 minutes so the flavors blend. Serve with chips.

Pantry swaps

  • No blender? Finely chop everything and use canned diced tomatoes.
  • Smoky? Add 1 canned chipotle pepper or a pinch of smoked paprika.

Rusty Chef tip: Salsa tastes better after an hour in the fridge, so make it ahead.

Storage

Keeps 1 week in the fridge.
