Swedish Meatballs

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 45 minutes · Swedish

Jump to recipe

Swedish meatballs are small, tender and gently spiced, smothered in a silky, creamy gravy.

Serve them the classic way with mashed potatoes, lingonberry or cranberry jam and a few pickles.

A pinch of allspice

Allspice or nutmeg is what makes these taste Swedish instead of Italian. Just 1/4 teaspoon gives them a warm, subtle flavor.

Swedish Meatballs

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total45 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.95/serving

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 lb ground beef (or half beef, half pork)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 onion, grated or very finely chopped
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice or nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1/2 cup sour cream or heavy cream
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • Lingonberry or cranberry jam, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the beef, egg, onion, allspice, salt and a few grinds of pepper. Mix gently with your hands just until combined.
  3. Roll into 1-inch balls. You should get about 24.
  4. Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Brown the meatballs in batches for 6 to 8 minutes, turning, until cooked through. Move them to a plate.
  5. Melt the remaining 2 tablespoons butter in the skillet. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute until lightly browned.
  6. Slowly whisk in the stock and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until thickened. Lower the heat and whisk in the sour cream and soy sauce.
  7. Return the meatballs to the pan and simmer for 2 minutes to coat. Serve with mashed potatoes and jam.

Pantry swaps

  • No allspice? Use a pinch of nutmeg, or leave it out.
  • No sour cream? Use heavy cream, or milk for a thinner gravy.

Rusty Chef tip: Wet your hands with cold water before rolling the meatballs so the mixture doesn't stick.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
