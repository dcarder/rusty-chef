One-Pan Beef Enchilada Skillet

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 35 minutes · Mexican & Tex-Mex

Jump to recipe

This skillet has everything you love about enchiladas without rolling a single tortilla.

The sauce is made from canned tomatoes and spices, so there's no need for a can of enchilada sauce.

Make the sauce from the pantry

Canned tomatoes simmered with chili powder, cumin and garlic make a quick red sauce that tastes a lot like the canned enchilada sauce.

One-Pan Beef Enchilada Skillet

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$3.02/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed or diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup canned or frozen corn (optional)
  • 6 small tortillas, cut into strips
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheese
  • Salt, to taste
  • Sour cream, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef and onion in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking up the meat. Drain off excess fat.
  2. Add the garlic, chili powder and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, beans, corn, 1/2 cup of water and a pinch of salt. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Heat the broiler. Stir the tortilla strips into the skillet and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until they soften.
  5. Sprinkle the cheese over the top and broil for 2 to 3 minutes until melted and bubbly. Serve with sour cream.

Pantry swaps

  • No ground beef? Use shredded chicken or an extra can of beans.
  • No oven-safe pan? Cover the skillet with a lid for 3 minutes on the stove to melt the cheese.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep an eye on it under the broiler. Cheese goes from melted to burnt in under a minute.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
