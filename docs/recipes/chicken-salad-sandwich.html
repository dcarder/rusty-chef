Classic Chicken Salad Sandwich

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 10 minutes · American

Jump to recipe

Chicken salad turns a little leftover or canned chicken into several days of lunches.

Serve it on bread, in a wrap, on crackers or over lettuce.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Whenever I cook chicken, I make a little extra just for this. A couple of cups of leftover chicken becomes lunch for days, which is a lifesaver when the week gets crazy. When it's just my wife and me, I keep a container in the fridge and grab it straight out for a quick sandwich.– Daniel

Add some crunch

Diced celery, onion or apple keep chicken salad from being just soft and creamy. That crunch is what makes it taste like the deli version.

Classic Chicken Salad Sandwich

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.23/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, chopped
  • 1/3 cup mayo
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 celery stalk, diced (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons diced onion (optional)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 8 slices bread

Instructions

  1. Mix the chicken, mayo and mustard in a bowl.
  2. Stir in the celery and onion.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Spread onto bread and add lettuce if you have it.
  5. Serve right away or chill until lunchtime.

Pantry swaps

  • Canned chicken? Drain it well and use it the same way.
  • Lighter? Swap half the mayo for plain yogurt.
  • Sweet twist? Add a handful of chopped grapes or apple.

Rusty Chef tip: A squeeze of lemon brightens it up and keeps it tasting fresh for days.

Storage

Chicken salad keeps 3 days in the fridge.
