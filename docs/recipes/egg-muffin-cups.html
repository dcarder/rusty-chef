Make-Ahead Egg Muffin Cups

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Egg muffin cups are like mini omelets baked in a muffin tin, and one batch covers breakfast for most of the week.

They reheat in under a minute, so busy mornings still get a hot, protein-packed breakfast.

Grease the tin well

Eggs stick like glue. Grease every cup generously with butter or oil, or use paper liners, and let the cups cool for 5 minutes before lifting them out.

Make-Ahead Egg Muffin Cups

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.28/serving

Ingredients

  • 10 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup diced ham
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • 1/2 bell pepper, finely diced (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Butter or oil, for the tin

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. Divide the ham, spinach, bell pepper, green onions and most of the cheese between the cups.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk and salt together, then pour into the cups until each is about three-quarters full.
  4. Sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
  5. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until puffed and set in the middle. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from the tin.

Pantry swaps

  • No ham? Cooked sausage, bacon or leave it out for a vegetarian version.
  • No spinach? Any chopped vegetable, such as frozen peas or diced onion.

Rusty Chef tip: The cups puff up in the oven and sink as they cool. That's normal, so don't overfill them.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer. Microwave for 30 to 60 seconds.
