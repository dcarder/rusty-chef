Diner-Style Smash Burgers

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 20 minutes · Bar Food

Jump to recipe

Smash burgers are what burger joints charge top dollar for, and they're actually easier to make than thick burgers.

Smashing the beef onto a very hot pan creates crispy, caramelized edges full of flavor.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Smash burgers taste like the ones from those trendy burger spots that charge fifteen bucks a burger. At home I make a stack of them in about ten minutes with a pound of ground beef. My kids love helping smash them, and my wife says that crispy edge is the best part.– Daniel

Smash once, early

Press the ball of beef flat in the first 30 seconds while it's still cold, then leave it alone. Smashing later squeezes out the juices.

Diner-Style Smash Burgers

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.27/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns, toasted
  • Sauce: 1/4 cup mayo, 2 tbsp ketchup, 1 tbsp pickle relish or chopped pickles
  • Thinly sliced onion (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix the sauce ingredients and set aside. Roll the beef into 8 loose balls.
  2. Heat a cast-iron or heavy skillet over high heat until very hot.
  3. Place 2 balls in the pan and smash flat with a sturdy spatula. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook for 2 minutes until the edges are crispy, flip, add cheese and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Stack two patties per bun with sauce and onion.

Pantry swaps

  • No cast iron? Any heavy skillet works; just get it very hot.
  • Leaner beef? Works, but 80/20 gives the best crispy edges.

Rusty Chef tip: Put a piece of parchment between the spatula and the beef so it doesn't stick when you smash.

Storage

Best fresh.
