Easy Classic Cheesecake

By Daniel Carder · Dessert · 75 minutes plus chilling · American

Jump to recipe

A homemade cheesecake looks impressive, but it's mostly cream cheese, sugar and eggs stirred together and baked low and slow.

This version skips the fussy water bath. A low oven and a slow cool-down keep it smooth and crack-free.

Start with soft cream cheese

Leave the cream cheese, sour cream and eggs on the counter for about an hour first. Cold cream cheese leaves lumps, and beating harder to get rid of them whips in air that makes the top crack.

Easy Classic Cheesecake

  • Prep20 min
  • Bake55 min
  • Chill4 hr
  • Serves12
  • Est. cost$0.95/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs (about 10 full sheets)
  • 1/4 cup sugar, for the crust
  • 6 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 3 blocks (8 oz each) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar, for the filling
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Stir the graham cracker crumbs, 1/4 cup sugar and melted butter together until it looks like wet sand.
  2. Press the crumbs firmly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan. Bake for 10 minutes, then set aside.
  3. Beat the cream cheese and 1 cup sugar on medium-low speed for 2 minutes until completely smooth. Mix in the sour cream, flour and vanilla.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low just until each one disappears. Don't overmix.
  5. Pour the filling over the crust and smooth the top. Bake for 50 to 55 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still jiggles slightly when you nudge the pan.
  6. Turn off the oven, crack the door open and leave the cheesecake inside for 1 hour.
  7. Run a thin knife around the edge, let it cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before slicing.

Swaps and toppings

  • No springform pan? Bake it in a 9x13 pan for 35 to 40 minutes and cut it into cheesecake bars.
  • No sour cream? Use plain Greek yogurt.
  • Want a topping? Spoon cherry pie filling, strawberries or warm chocolate sauce over each slice.

Rusty Chef tip: For clean slices, dip your knife in hot water and wipe it dry between every cut.

Storage

Keeps for 5 days in the fridge, covered. Freeze whole or in slices for up to 2 months and thaw overnight in the fridge.
