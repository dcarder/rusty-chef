Easy Beef and Broccoli

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 25 minutes · Chinese

Jump to recipe

Beef and broccoli is a Chinese takeout staple, and the sauce is just soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic and ginger.

Tossing the beef in a little cornstarch keeps it tender and helps the sauce cling, the same trick restaurants use.

Slice against the grain

Look for the lines running through the steak and cut across them in thin strips. Putting the steak in the freezer for 15 minutes first makes thin slicing much easier.

Easy Beef and Broccoli

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.40/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak or sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch, divided
  • 4 tablespoons soy sauce, divided
  • 4 cups broccoli florets, fresh or frozen
  • 2 tablespoons oil, divided
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger or 1 tablespoon fresh, grated
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup beef or chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • Cooked rice, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Toss the sliced beef with 1 tablespoon cornstarch and 1 tablespoon soy sauce. Let it sit while you prep everything else.
  2. In a small bowl, stir together the remaining 3 tablespoons soy sauce, the brown sugar, stock, sesame oil and remaining 1 tablespoon cornstarch.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add the broccoli and 2 tablespoons water, cover and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until bright green and just tender. Move to a plate.
  4. Add the remaining oil. Spread the beef in one layer and sear for 1 to 2 minutes per side until browned. Work in batches if needed so it browns instead of steaming.
  5. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds. Stir the sauce, pour it in and simmer for 1 minute until thick and glossy.
  6. Return the broccoli, toss everything to coat and serve over rice.

Pantry swaps

  • No flank steak? Any thinly sliced steak works, or use chicken breast.
  • Using frozen broccoli? Skip the steaming step and add it straight to the sauce until hot.

Rusty Chef tip: Cook the beef in a hot pan without crowding it. Too much at once and it turns gray instead of browning.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet with a splash of water.
