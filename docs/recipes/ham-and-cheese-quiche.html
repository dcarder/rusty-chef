Easy Ham and Cheese Quiche

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 60 minutes

Jump to recipe

Quiche looks fancy but it's really just eggs, milk and cheese baked in a simple pie crust.

It's just as good cold the next day, which makes it a great make-ahead lunch.

Bake the crust first

Baking the empty crust for 10 minutes before adding the filling keeps the bottom crisp instead of soggy.

Easy Ham and Cheese Quiche

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook40 min
  • Total60 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.03/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup cold butter, cubed
  • 3 tablespoons cold water
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 cup diced ham
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)
  • A handful of spinach (optional)

Instructions

  1. Rub the butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs. Stir in the cold water until the dough holds together, then chill for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Roll the dough out and press it into a 9-inch pie dish. Prick the bottom with a fork and bake for 10 minutes.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk and salt together.
  4. Scatter the ham, cheese and any extras over the crust, then pour in the egg mixture.
  5. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the center is just set with a slight wobble. Cool for 10 minutes before slicing.

Pantry swaps

  • Short on time? Use a store-bought pie crust, or skip the crust and bake it in a greased dish for a crustless quiche.
  • No ham? Use bacon, sausage or sautéed vegetables.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep the butter and water very cold. Cold butter makes a flaky crust.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Eat cold or reheat slices in a low oven.
