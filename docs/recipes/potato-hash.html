Crispy Potato Hash With Eggs

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

Potato hash is a hearty one-pan breakfast that also makes a great cheap dinner.

Crispy potatoes, sweet onions and eggs cooked right on top. Add leftover meat or vegetables if you have them.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Potatoes, an onion and a few eggs make one of my favorite cheap meals. I make it for breakfast on weekends and for dinner when the budget is tight. The crispy edges are everybody's favorite part, so I let the potatoes sit in the pan longer than you'd think.– Daniel

Par-cook the potatoes

Microwaving the diced potatoes for 4 to 5 minutes first means they only need to crisp in the pan, cutting the cooking time in half.

Crispy Potato Hash With Eggs

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves3

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs potatoes, diced small
  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • 3 to 4 eggs
  • Hot sauce (optional)

Instructions

  1. Microwave the potatoes in a covered bowl for 4 to 5 minutes until just tender.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the potatoes in a single layer.
  3. Cook undisturbed for 5 minutes, then flip and add the onion, paprika, salt and pepper. Cook 8 to 10 minutes more, stirring occasionally, until crisp.
  4. Make wells in the hash and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook on low for 4 to 5 minutes until the whites are set.
  5. Serve with hot sauce.

Pantry swaps

  • No paprika? Chili powder or garlic powder.
  • Sweet potatoes? Work great; cook a little less.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't stir too often. Potatoes need time in contact with the hot pan to form a crust.

Storage

The hash keeps 3 days. Reheat in a skillet and cook fresh eggs.
