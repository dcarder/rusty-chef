Fluffy Pantry Pancakes

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

You don't need a boxed mix for great pancakes. A few staples whisked together make a stack that's light, fluffy and golden.

This recipe makes about 10 medium pancakes. Serve with butter and syrup, or fold in chocolate chips or blueberries.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Saturday morning pancakes are a tradition in our house, and I stopped buying the boxed mix a long time ago. It's just a few staples you already have. My kids like to help stir while my wife handles the toppings, and a stack of these costs less than a single fast-food breakfast.– Daniel

Lumps are good

Stir the batter just until no dry flour remains. Overmixing develops gluten and makes pancakes tough and flat. A few small lumps will disappear as they cook.

Fluffy Pantry Pancakes

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$0.36/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir gently until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased skillet over medium heat. Pour in 1/4 cup batter per pancake.
  5. Flip when bubbles cover the surface and the edges look set, about 2 minutes. Cook 1 more minute.

Pantry swaps

  • No butter? Use 3 tablespoons of oil.
  • No eggs? Use 1/4 cup mashed banana or plain yogurt.

Rusty Chef tip: Let the batter rest for 5 minutes before cooking. The baking powder gets a head start and the pancakes rise higher.

Storage

Cooled pancakes keep 3 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer. Reheat in a toaster.
