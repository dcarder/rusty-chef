Ham and Cheese Pinwheels

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 15 minutes

Jump to recipe

Pinwheels are a no-cook lunch that kids and adults both grab by the handful.

They take 15 minutes to make and travel well, so they're great for lunch boxes and parties.

Chill before slicing

A tightly rolled tortilla that has spent 30 minutes in the fridge slices into neat rounds. Sliced straight away, it tends to squish and unroll.

Ham and Cheese Pinwheels

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.64/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 6 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 8 thin slices ham
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 teaspoon mustard (optional)
  • 1 cup baby spinach (optional)
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder (optional)

Instructions

  1. Stir the cream cheese with the mustard and garlic powder, if using, until smooth.
  2. Spread a thin layer over each tortilla, right to the edges.
  3. Lay 2 slices of ham on each, then sprinkle with cheese and add a few spinach leaves.
  4. Roll each tortilla up tightly and wrap in plastic wrap. Chill for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Trim off the ends and cut each roll into 1-inch slices.

Pantry swaps

  • No ham? Turkey, salami or pepperoni.
  • No cream cheese? Use mayo or hummus as the spread.

Rusty Chef tip: Spread the cream cheese all the way to the edge of the tortilla. It works as glue and keeps the roll sealed.

Storage

Keeps for 2 days in the fridge, wrapped tightly.
