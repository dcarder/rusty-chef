Crispy Fried Eggs

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 5 minutes

Jump to recipe

Crispy fried eggs have golden, lacy edges and soft, runny yolks, and they take less than 5 minutes.

Put them on toast, rice, beans or leftover noodles to turn almost anything into a meal.

Hot oil is the trick

For crispy edges, heat the oil until it shimmers before the eggs go in. Spooning the hot oil over the whites cooks the tops without flipping the eggs.

Crispy Fried Eggs

  • Prep1 min
  • Cook4 min
  • Total5 min
  • Serves1
  • Est. cost$0.75/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons oil or butter
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of chili flakes (optional)
  • Toast, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a small skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers.
  2. Crack the eggs into the pan. They should sizzle and bubble right away.
  3. Cook for 1 minute, then tilt the pan and spoon the hot oil over the whites, avoiding the yolks.
  4. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes more, until the edges are brown and crispy and the whites are set.
  5. Season with salt and chili flakes and serve on toast.

Pantry swaps

  • Prefer firm yolks? Flip the eggs and cook for 1 minute more.
  • No oil? Butter works, but use medium heat so it doesn't burn.

Rusty Chef tip: Crack each egg into a small cup first. It's easier to slide them gently into the hot oil and catch any bits of shell.

Storage

Best eaten right away.
