Crispy Roasted Chickpeas

By Daniel Carder · Snack · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

Roasted chickpeas are a crunchy, savory snack that costs pennies to make.

Season them any way you like: smoky, spicy, garlicky or even sweet with cinnamon and sugar.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I started making these as a cheap snack instead of buying chips. One can of chickpeas turns into a whole bowl of crunchy bites. My kids like them with a little cinnamon sugar, my wife likes them garlicky, and I like mine spicy.– Daniel

Dry them thoroughly

Moisture is the enemy of crispiness. Pat the chickpeas very dry with a towel and peel off any loose skins before roasting.

Crispy Roasted Chickpeas

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook30 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Rinse the chickpeas and dry them thoroughly with a towel.
  3. Toss with oil and spread on a baking sheet in a single layer.
  4. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, shaking halfway, until deep golden and crunchy.
  5. Toss with the paprika, garlic powder and salt while still hot.

Pantry swaps

  • No paprika? Cumin, curry powder or chili powder.
  • Air fryer? Cook at 390°F for 12 to 15 minutes.

Rusty Chef tip: Add seasonings after roasting. Spices can burn in a hot oven.

Storage

Best the day they're made. They soften in a sealed container.
