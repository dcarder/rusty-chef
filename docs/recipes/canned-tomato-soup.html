Easy Tomato Soup From Canned Tomatoes

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Canned tomatoes are picked and packed at peak ripeness, which is why they often make better soup than out-of-season fresh ones.

This version is smooth, slightly sweet and perfect with a grilled cheese sandwich. It takes about half an hour, mostly hands-off.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Nothing beats a pot of this with grilled cheese on a rainy Florida afternoon. I used to buy canned soup until I realized I could make a whole pot of better soup from one can of tomatoes. My kids dip their sandwiches in it, and there's never any left.– Daniel

Balance the acidity

Canned tomatoes can be sharp. A small pinch of sugar and a knob of butter round out the acidity without making the soup taste sweet.

Easy Tomato Soup From Canned Tomatoes

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 can (28 oz) whole or crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups stock or water
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Salt and pepper
  • Splash of cream or milk (optional)

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat. Cook the onion for 6 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Blend until smooth with an immersion blender, or carefully in batches in a regular blender.
  5. Stir in cream or milk if using, and season with salt and pepper.

Pantry swaps

  • No stock? Water plus an extra pinch of salt works fine.
  • No butter? Use olive oil.
  • No blender? Use crushed tomatoes and leave the soup rustic and chunky.

Rusty Chef tip: Add a parmesan rind while the soup simmers and remove it before blending. It adds a deep, savory flavor for free.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
