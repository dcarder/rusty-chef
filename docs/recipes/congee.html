Savory Rice Porridge (Congee)

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 50 minutes

Jump to recipe

Congee is a soothing rice porridge eaten across Asia for breakfast or whenever comfort is needed.

A small amount of rice cooked in lots of liquid becomes silky and creamy. Then it's all about the toppings.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

When I'm under the weather or just need something comforting, I make a pot of this rice porridge. It's cheap, gentle on your stomach and endlessly customizable. A little soy sauce and a soft egg on top, and I feel better already.– Daniel

Use leftover rice to save time

Cooked rice breaks down in about 20 minutes instead of 45. Use 1 1/2 cups cooked rice and 5 cups liquid.

Savory Rice Porridge (Congee)

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook45 min
  • Total50 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup rice
  • 7 cups stock or water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Soy sauce
  • Soft-boiled or fried eggs (optional)
  • Sliced green onion (optional)
  • Sesame oil (optional)

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice and add it to a large pot with the stock and salt.
  2. Bring to a boil, then simmer partly covered for 40 to 45 minutes, stirring often.
  3. Cook until the rice breaks down into a thick, creamy porridge. Add water if it gets too thick.
  4. Ladle into bowls.
  5. Top with soy sauce, egg, green onion and a few drops of sesame oil.

Pantry swaps

  • No stock? Water with a little extra salt and a slice of ginger.
  • Want protein? Stir in shredded leftover chicken.

Rusty Chef tip: Stir well near the end. The rice sinks and can stick to the bottom of the pot.

Storage

Keeps 4 days. It thickens a lot, so add water when reheating.
