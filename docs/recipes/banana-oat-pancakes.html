Banana Oat Pancakes

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

These pancakes are made by blending oats, bananas, eggs and milk, so there's no flour and almost no added sugar.

They're a great way to use up spotty bananas and they freeze really well.

Let the batter rest

Give the blended batter 5 minutes before cooking. The oats soak up some liquid and the batter thickens, which makes fluffier pancakes.

Banana Oat Pancakes

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$0.55/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • Butter, for the pan
  • Honey or syrup, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Put the oats, bananas, eggs, milk, baking powder, cinnamon and salt in a blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes to thicken.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium-low heat and add a little butter.
  4. Pour in 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles form and the edges look set, then flip and cook for 1 to 2 minutes more.
  5. Serve warm with sliced banana and a drizzle of honey or syrup.

Pantry swaps

  • No blender? Use quick oats, mash the bananas well and whisk everything together.
  • Want berries? Drop a few blueberries onto each pancake before flipping.

Rusty Chef tip: Cook these a little lower and slower than regular pancakes. The banana makes them brown faster.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer. Reheat in the toaster.
