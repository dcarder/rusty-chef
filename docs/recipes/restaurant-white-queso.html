Mexican Restaurant White Queso

By Daniel Carder · Snack · 15 minutes

Jump to recipe

White queso is the dip everybody fights over at Mexican restaurants, and it costs a lot for a small bowl.

The secret ingredient is white American cheese from the deli counter. It melts perfectly smooth.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

The queso at Mexican restaurants is the first thing my wife and kids ask for, and those little bowls cost a fortune. The trick I learned is white American cheese from the deli counter: it melts perfectly smooth every time. We make a pot of this for movie nights and game days.– Daniel

Use deli American cheese

Sliced white American cheese melts silky smooth without turning grainy or oily. Cheddar alone separates; American keeps it creamy.

Mexican Restaurant White Queso

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.51/serving

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb white American cheese, torn into pieces
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 can (4 oz) diced green chiles, or 2 tablespoons salsa
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • Pinch of garlic powder (optional)
  • Tortilla chips, to serve

Instructions

  1. Warm the milk and butter in a small pot over low heat.
  2. Add the cheese a handful at a time, stirring until melted and smooth.
  3. Stir in the green chiles, cumin and garlic powder.
  4. If it's too thick, add a splash more milk.
  5. Serve warm with tortilla chips.

Pantry swaps

  • No American cheese? Use half Monterey Jack plus 1 teaspoon cornstarch tossed with the cheese.
  • Spicier? Add a diced jalapeño or a few dashes of hot sauce.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep the heat low the whole time. High heat makes cheese dip grainy.

Storage

Keeps 4 days. Reheat on low or in the microwave with a splash of milk, stirring often.
