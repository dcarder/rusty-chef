Easy Irish Soda Bread

By Daniel Carder · Bread · 50 minutes · Irish

Jump to recipe

Irish soda bread is the fastest real bread you can make: no yeast, no kneading and no waiting for dough to rise.

Baking soda and buttermilk do all the work, and it's out of the oven in under an hour.

Make your own buttermilk

No buttermilk? Stir 1 tablespoon of vinegar or lemon juice into 1 3/4 cups of milk and let it sit for 5 minutes until it thickens slightly. It works just as well.

Easy Irish Soda Bread

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook40 min
  • Total50 min
  • Serves8
  • Est. cost$0.30/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (optional)
  • 1 3/4 cups buttermilk, or milk mixed with 1 tablespoon vinegar

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or dust it with flour.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, salt and sugar together in a large bowl.
  3. Pour in the buttermilk and stir with a fork until a shaggy dough forms.
  4. Turn the dough onto a floured counter and pat it gently into a round about 2 inches thick. Don't knead it much.
  5. Set it on the baking sheet and cut a deep X across the top with a sharp knife.
  6. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until golden brown and the bottom sounds hollow when you tap it.
  7. Cool on a rack for at least 20 minutes before slicing. Wrap it in a towel while it cools if you like a softer crust.

Pantry swaps

  • Want it sweeter? Add a cup of raisins and a tablespoon of sugar to make what the Irish call "spotted dog."
  • Want it heartier? Swap half the flour for whole wheat flour.

Rusty Chef tip: Handle the dough as little as possible. The less you work it, the more tender the bread.

Storage

Best the day it's baked. Keeps 2 days wrapped at room temperature and makes great toast. Freezes for 3 months.
