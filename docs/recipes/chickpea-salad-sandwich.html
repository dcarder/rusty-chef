Smashed Chickpea Salad Sandwich

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

Think tuna salad, but made with chickpeas. It's a cheap, plant-based lunch that's ready in 10 minutes.

Pile it into a sandwich, wrap it in a tortilla or eat it with crackers.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I started making this when I wanted a cheap lunch that wasn't another peanut butter sandwich. It tastes a lot like tuna salad but costs even less. I keep a container in the fridge, and it makes lunches easy for a few days.– Daniel

Leave some texture

Mash the chickpeas roughly with a fork so some stay in chunks. A smooth paste makes for a mushy sandwich.

Smashed Chickpea Salad Sandwich

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves3

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
  • 3 tablespoons mayo or yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Diced onion or celery (optional)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 6 slices bread

Instructions

  1. Mash the chickpeas roughly with a fork.
  2. Stir in the mayo, mustard, lemon juice and onion.
  3. Season well with salt and pepper.
  4. Spread on bread and add lettuce or tomato if you have them.

Pantry swaps

  • No mayo? Plain yogurt or a spoonful of hummus.
  • No lemon? Pickle juice or vinegar.

Rusty Chef tip: Toast the bread. It adds crunch and keeps the sandwich from getting soggy.

Storage

The filling keeps 4 days in the fridge.
