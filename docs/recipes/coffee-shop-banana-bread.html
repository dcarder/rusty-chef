Coffee Shop Banana Bread

By Daniel Carder · Bread · 70 minutes

Jump to recipe

Coffee shops charge as much for one slice of banana bread as it costs to bake a whole loaf at home.

This one-bowl recipe is the best way to use bananas that are too spotty to eat.

Riper is better

Bananas with lots of brown or black spots are sweeter and mash more easily, so the bread is moister and more flavorful. If yours aren't ready, bake them in their peels at 300°F for 15 minutes until black.

Coffee Shop Banana Bread

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook60 min
  • Total70 min
  • Serves10
  • Est. cost$0.24/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl until smooth. Stir in the melted butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
  3. Add the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt and stir just until no dry flour remains. Don't overmix.
  4. Pour into the pan and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean. Cover loosely with foil after 40 minutes if the top is browning too fast.
  5. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely before slicing.

Pantry swaps

  • Want mix-ins? Fold in 1/2 cup chocolate chips or chopped nuts.
  • No butter? Use the same amount of oil.

Rusty Chef tip: Lumpy batter is good. Overmixing makes banana bread tough and dense.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days at room temperature, wrapped, or 3 months in the freezer. Freeze it sliced so you can toast one piece at a time.
