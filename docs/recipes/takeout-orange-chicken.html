Better-Than-Takeout Orange Chicken

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 35 minutes · Chinese

Jump to recipe

Orange chicken is the most popular order at Chinese fast-food chains, and it's easy to make at home.

This version pan-fries the chicken in a little oil instead of deep-frying, and the sauce takes 5 minutes.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Orange chicken was our family's go-to takeout order, and it was eating up our budget. I finally figured out how to get that crispy coating at home with a little cornstarch and a skillet. Now my wife and kids ask for my orange chicken instead of the drive-thru, and that's a win in every way.– Daniel

Cornstarch makes it crispy

Tossing the chicken in cornstarch before frying gives it a light, crispy coating that holds up in the sauce, just like takeout.

Better-Than-Takeout Orange Chicken

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.70/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs boneless chicken thighs, cut in bite-size pieces
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch, plus 1 tablespoon for the sauce
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 3/4 cup orange juice
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger (optional)
  • Cooked rice

Instructions

  1. Dip the chicken in the egg, then toss in 1/2 cup cornstarch until coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the chicken in batches for 5 to 6 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towels.
  3. Wipe out the pan. Add the orange juice, sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and ginger and bring to a simmer.
  4. Mix 1 tablespoon cornstarch with 2 tablespoons water, stir it in and simmer for 1 minute until thick and glossy.
  5. Toss the chicken in the sauce and serve over rice.

Pantry swaps

  • No orange juice? Use 1/2 cup water plus 3 tablespoons orange marmalade.
  • Air fryer? Spray the coated chicken with oil and air fry at 400°F for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking halfway.
  • Spicy? Add a pinch of chili flakes to the sauce.

Rusty Chef tip: Toss the chicken in the sauce right before serving so the coating stays crispy.

Storage

Keeps 3 days. Reheat in a hot skillet or air fryer to crisp it back up.
