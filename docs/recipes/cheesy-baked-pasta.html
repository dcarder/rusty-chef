Cheesy Baked Pasta

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 45 minutes

Jump to recipe

Baked pasta is like lasagna without the layering, and it feeds a family from one dish.

Make it with plain tomato sauce, or brown some sausage first for a heartier dinner.

Undercook the pasta

Boil the pasta for 2 minutes less than the package says. It keeps cooking in the oven and stays firm instead of going soft.

Cheesy Baked Pasta

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook35 min
  • Total45 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.53/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb short pasta, such as penne or ziti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella or other cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan (optional)
  • 1/2 lb Italian sausage, casings removed (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Cook the pasta in salted water for 2 minutes less than the package says, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Brown the sausage, if using, then add the onion and cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, herbs and salt and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir the pasta into the sauce with half the mozzarella, then spread it in a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  5. Top with the rest of the mozzarella and the parmesan. Bake for 20 minutes until bubbling and golden.

Pantry swaps

  • No mozzarella? Any melting cheese works, such as cheddar or a pizza blend.
  • Want it creamier? Stir a few spoonfuls of cream cheese or ricotta into the sauce.

Rusty Chef tip: Let it rest for 5 minutes out of the oven. It firms up and is much easier to serve.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
