Chicken Enchilada Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 35 minutes · Mexican & Tex-Mex

Jump to recipe

This soup tastes like a pan of chicken enchiladas in a bowl: smoky, cheesy and creamy.

It's mostly cans from the pantry plus leftover or rotisserie chicken, and it's ready in about 35 minutes.

The rotisserie chicken shortcut

A store rotisserie chicken gives you about 3 cups of shredded meat and saves you 20 minutes. Raw chicken works too: simmer 2 chicken breasts whole in the soup for 15 to 20 minutes, then pull them out, shred them with two forks and stir them back in.

Chicken Enchilada Soup

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.60/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced (optional)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 can (10 oz) red enchilada sauce
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn, frozen or canned
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 4 oz cream cheese, cubed
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or Mexican-blend cheese
  • Salt, to taste
  • Tortilla chips, sour cream and green onions, for topping (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, chili powder and cumin and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the stock, enchilada sauce, tomatoes, black beans and corn. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in the chicken and cream cheese and keep stirring for 2 to 3 minutes until the cream cheese melts into the soup.
  5. Turn the heat to low and stir in the shredded cheese a handful at a time until melted and smooth. Taste and add salt.
  6. Ladle into bowls and top with crushed tortilla chips, sour cream and green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No enchilada sauce? Use one 8-oz can of tomato sauce plus 1 more tablespoon of chili powder.
  • Like it spicy? Add a diced jalapeño with the onion or a small can of green chiles with the beans.
  • Want it lighter? Skip the cream cheese. The soup will be brothier but still full of flavor.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep the heat low when you add the cheese. If the soup is boiling, the cheese can clump instead of melting smooth.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer. The cheese can separate a little after freezing, so stir it well as it reheats.
