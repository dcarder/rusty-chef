Bangers and Mash with Onion Gravy

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 45 minutes · French & European

Jump to recipe

Bangers and mash is classic British pub food: juicy sausages on a pile of buttery mashed potatoes, smothered in rich onion gravy.

It's simple, cheap and on the table in about 45 minutes.

Cook the sausages low and slow

Bangers got their name from bursting open with a bang in a hot pan. Medium-low heat browns them evenly without splitting the skins.

Bangers and Mash with Onion Gravy

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook30 min
  • Total45 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.85/serving

Ingredients

  • 8 pork sausages (bangers)
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 1/2 lbs potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 4 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 1/2 cup milk, warmed
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire or soy sauce
  • Frozen peas, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Put the potatoes in a pot, cover with cold salted water and bring to a boil. Simmer for about 15 minutes until very tender.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Cook the sausages for 15 to 20 minutes, turning often, until browned all over and cooked through. Move them to a plate and keep warm.
  3. In the same pan, melt 2 tablespoons butter. Add the onions, sugar and a pinch of salt and cook for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and golden.
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the stock and Worcestershire, then simmer for 3 to 5 minutes until thick and glossy.
  5. Drain the potatoes and mash them with the remaining butter and the warm milk. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Pile the mash on plates, top with the sausages and smother everything in onion gravy. Serve with peas.

Pantry swaps

  • Can't find British bangers? Any mild pork sausage works, including bratwurst or mild Italian.
  • No Worcestershire? Use soy sauce for the same savory depth.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't poke holes in the sausages. It lets the juices run out and dries them out.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Store the gravy separately and reheat it on the stove.
