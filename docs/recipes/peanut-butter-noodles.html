15-Minute Peanut Butter Noodles

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 15 minutes

Jump to recipe

That jar of peanut butter is secretly one of the best sauce bases in your pantry. Whisked with soy sauce, garlic and a little acid, it becomes a rich, nutty noodle sauce.

Eat these warm or cold. They make a great packed lunch the next day.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This one was a win with my pickiest eater. My kids already love peanut butter and my wife loves anything with noodles, so turning it into a noodle sauce was an easy sell. I keep the ingredients on hand for nights when I'm wiped out and have about fifteen minutes of energy left.– Daniel

Thin the sauce with hot water

Peanut butter seizes up when it's cold. Whisking in hot water a tablespoon at a time makes the sauce smooth and pourable so it coats every noodle.

15-Minute Peanut Butter Noodles

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$0.71/serving

Ingredients

  • 6 oz noodles or spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar or lime juice
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1 teaspoon honey or sugar
  • Chili flakes or sriracha (optional)
  • Sliced green onion (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles according to the package. Save 1/2 cup cooking water, then drain.
  2. Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and honey in a large bowl.
  3. Whisk in hot noodle water a tablespoon at a time until smooth and pourable.
  4. Toss the noodles in the sauce.
  5. Top with chili and green onion.

Pantry swaps

  • No peanut butter? Almond butter, sunflower seed butter or tahini all work.
  • No vinegar? Lemon juice or a splash of orange juice.
  • No honey? Sugar, maple syrup or brown sugar.

Rusty Chef tip: Add frozen peas or shredded carrots to the pasta water for the last minute of cooking for an easy veg boost.

Storage

Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Loosen with a splash of water before eating.
