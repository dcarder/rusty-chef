One-Pot Stovetop Mac and Cheese

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 17 minutes

Jump to recipe

This mac and cheese cooks the pasta right in milk and water, so the starch thickens the sauce and you only wash one pot.

It's creamier than the boxed stuff and almost as fast.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

My picky kids would eat mac and cheese every single night if I let them. Making it in one pot from scratch is barely more work than the box, it's creamier, and I know exactly what's in it. Plus there's only one pot to wash, which matters on a long day.– Daniel

Take it off the heat before adding cheese

Cheese added over high heat can turn grainy and oily. Turn off the burner, then stir in the cheese a handful at a time until smooth.

One-Pot Stovetop Mac and Cheese

  • Prep2 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total17 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 8 oz elbow pasta
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard (optional)
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Add the pasta, milk, water and a pinch of salt to a pot.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring often so it doesn't stick.
  3. Cook for 10 to 12 minutes until the pasta is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
  4. Turn off the heat. Stir in the butter, mustard and cheese until smooth.
  5. Season with pepper and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No cheddar? Any melting cheese works, or a mix of leftovers.
  • No mustard? Skip it, or add a pinch of paprika.

Rusty Chef tip: Mustard won't make it taste mustardy. It sharpens the cheese flavor.

Storage

Keeps 3 days. Reheat with a splash of milk.
