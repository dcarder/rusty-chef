Chinese Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 15 minutes · Chinese

Jump to recipe

Tomato and egg stir-fry is one of the most-loved comfort foods in Chinese home cooking.

It's cheap, ready in 15 minutes and made from eggs and a can of tomatoes.

Pull the eggs early

Take the eggs out of the pan while they still look a little wet. They finish cooking when they go back into the hot tomato sauce, so they stay soft and fluffy.

Chinese Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.10/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, divided
  • 2 tablespoons oil, divided
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, or 3 ripe tomatoes cut into wedges
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup (optional)
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water
  • 2 green onions, sliced, white and green parts separated
  • Cooked rice, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs with 1/4 teaspoon salt.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Pour in the eggs and gently push them around until just set but still soft, about 1 minute. Move to a plate.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the white parts of the green onions. Cook for 30 seconds, then add the tomatoes, ketchup, sugar, soy sauce and remaining salt.
  4. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, pressing on the tomatoes, until saucy. Stir in the cornstarch mixture and cook for 30 seconds until glossy.
  5. Return the eggs, fold gently and top with the green onion tops. Serve over rice.

Pantry swaps

  • Have fresh tomatoes? Use 3 ripe ones cut into wedges and add a splash of water.
  • Like it spicy? Add a pinch of chili flakes with the green onions.

Rusty Chef tip: A spoonful of ketchup boosts the tomato flavor, especially when fresh tomatoes are out of season.

Storage

Best eaten fresh. Keeps for 2 days in the fridge.
