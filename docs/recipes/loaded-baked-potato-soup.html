Loaded Baked Potato Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 45 minutes · American

Jump to recipe

Everything you love on a loaded baked potato goes into this soup: bacon, cheddar, sour cream and green onions.

It's a thick, steakhouse-style soup made from a bag of potatoes and a few fridge basics.

Microwave the potatoes

Baked potatoes give this soup its fluffy, roasted flavor, and the microwave gets you there in about 10 minutes. Chop up a little of the skin and stir it in for real baked-potato taste.

Loaded Baked Potato Soup

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook35 min
  • Total45 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.40/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs russet potatoes (about 4 large), scrubbed
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar, divided
  • Salt and pepper
  • 3 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Poke the potatoes all over with a fork. Microwave for 10 to 12 minutes, turning halfway, until fork-tender. (Or bake at 400°F for about 1 hour.) Let them cool a bit.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the bacon in a large pot over medium heat for 6 to 8 minutes until crisp. Move it to a paper towel and leave about 2 tablespoons of the fat in the pot.
  3. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then the garlic for 30 seconds. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Slowly whisk in the stock, then the milk. Simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until it thickens.
  5. Cut the potatoes in half and scoop the insides into the pot, adding a little chopped skin if you like. Mash with a potato masher, leaving some chunks. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the sour cream and 1 cup of the cheddar until melted. Season well with salt and pepper.
  7. Ladle into bowls and top with the rest of the cheddar, the bacon and green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No bacon? Use 2 tablespoons butter to cook the onion and top the soup with chopped ham.
  • Too thick or too thin? Stir in more stock to thin it, or simmer a few minutes longer to thicken it.
  • No sour cream? Use plain Greek yogurt.

Rusty Chef tip: Use russet potatoes if you can. Their fluffy, starchy insides thicken the soup naturally.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Reheat gently with a splash of milk. It doesn't freeze well, because the potatoes and dairy turn grainy.
