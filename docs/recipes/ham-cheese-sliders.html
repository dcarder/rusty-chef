Baked Ham and Cheese Sliders

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Ham and cheese sliders are a party favorite that's easy and cheap to make in a big batch.

A simple butter-mustard topping makes them taste special.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

These are my go-to for game days and birthday parties because one pan feeds a crowd for cheap. My wife and kids devour them, and the kids love helping brush on the butter. I put them together ahead of time so I'm not stuck in the kitchen when everybody shows up.– Daniel

Slice the rolls in one piece

Keep the rolls connected and slice the whole slab in half sideways. Filling one big sandwich is much faster than making twelve little ones.

Baked Ham and Cheese Sliders

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.60/serving

Ingredients

  • 12 small dinner rolls
  • 8 oz sliced ham
  • 6 slices cheese
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 tablespoon mustard
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Slice the rolls in half sideways and put the bottoms in a baking dish.
  3. Layer the ham and cheese, then add the tops.
  4. Mix the butter, mustard and garlic powder and brush it over the rolls.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 10 minutes, then uncover for 5 minutes until golden.

Pantry swaps

  • No ham? Use turkey or leftover chicken.
  • No rolls? Use sandwich bread cut into quarters.

Rusty Chef tip: Assemble them ahead and keep them in the fridge. Bake right before serving.

Storage

Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Reheat covered at 350°F for 10 minutes.
