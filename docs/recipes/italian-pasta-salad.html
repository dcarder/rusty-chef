Italian Pasta Salad

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Pasta salad is a cheap make-ahead lunch that gets better as it sits in the fridge.

Use any vegetables, cheese or deli meat you have on hand.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I make a big bowl of this on Sunday and it carries us through the week. One box of pasta and a few things from the fridge turn into a bunch of lunches for just a few dollars. It's perfect for hot Florida days when nobody wants anything heavy, and it tastes even better on day two.– Daniel

Dress it while it's warm

Toss the pasta with the dressing while it's still warm. Warm pasta soaks up more flavor, so every bite is seasoned instead of just coated.

Italian Pasta Salad

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves8
  • Est. cost$0.36/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb short pasta
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced (optional)
  • 1/2 cup olives, sliced (optional)
  • 1 cup cheese, cubed (optional)
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta, drain and rinse briefly with cool water.
  2. Whisk the oil, vinegar, herbs, garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the warm pasta with most of the dressing.
  4. Add the pepper, olives and cheese.
  5. Chill for at least 30 minutes. Stir in the rest of the dressing before serving.

Pantry swaps

  • Bottled dressing? 1 cup Italian dressing works in place of the homemade one.
  • Add protein? Salami, ham, chickpeas or tuna all work.
  • No bell pepper? Use cucumber, cherry tomatoes or frozen peas.

Rusty Chef tip: Pack it in individual containers on Sunday for grab-and-go lunches all week.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in the fridge.
