Pantry Veggie Fried Noodles

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 17 minutes

Jump to recipe

Like fried rice, fried noodles are a brilliant way to use up whatever's in the freezer and fridge.

Spaghetti works surprisingly well if you don't have Asian-style noodles.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

When I'm out of rice but still craving takeout, I make these with whatever noodles I have. Even plain spaghetti works. It's how my wife and I sneak more vegetables to our picky kids, because they're too busy slurping noodles to notice.– Daniel

Rinse and dry the noodles

Rinsing cooked noodles in cold water removes surface starch so they don't clump in the pan. Shake them dry before frying.

Pantry Veggie Fried Noodles

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook12 min
  • Total17 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.02/serving

Ingredients

  • 6 oz noodles or spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 1/2 cups frozen vegetables
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 eggs (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sugar or honey
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles, rinse with cold water and drain well.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, sugar and sesame oil.
  3. Heat the oil over high heat. Stir-fry the vegetables and garlic for 3 minutes.
  4. Push to the side, scramble the eggs, then add the noodles and sauce.
  5. Toss for 2 minutes until hot and glossy.

Pantry swaps

  • No eggs? Add tofu or chickpeas.
  • Want heat? Add chili flakes or sriracha.

Rusty Chef tip: Cabbage, onions and carrots all work in place of frozen vegetables.

Storage

Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
