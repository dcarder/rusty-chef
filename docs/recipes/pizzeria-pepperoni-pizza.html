Pizzeria-Style Pepperoni Pizza

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

A delivered large pepperoni pizza now costs around $20. Making one at home costs less than the tip.

Store-bought dough, a quick no-cook sauce and a very hot oven get you surprisingly close to the pizzeria.

Crank up the heat

Pizzerias bake at 700°F or more. Your home oven can't get that hot, so heat it as high as it goes for a full 30 minutes, and bake the pizza on the lowest rack so the bottom crisps.

Pizzeria-Style Pepperoni Pizza

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.94/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough, at room temperature
  • 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1 clove garlic, grated, or a pinch of garlic powder
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 3 oz sliced pepperoni
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Grated parmesan, to serve (optional)
  • Chili flakes, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 500°F (260°C) or as high as it goes, with a baking sheet or pizza stone on the lowest rack, for 30 minutes.
  2. Stir the crushed tomatoes, herbs, garlic and salt together. That's your sauce.
  3. Stretch the dough into a 12- to 14-inch round on a sheet of parchment paper. Brush the edge with olive oil.
  4. Spread the sauce thinly over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border, then add the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  5. Slide the pizza, still on the parchment, onto the hot baking sheet. Bake for 10 to 14 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling. Rest for 2 minutes before slicing.

Pantry swaps

  • No pizza dough? Use naan, flatbread or flour tortillas for a thin, crispy pizza in 8 minutes.
  • Not a pepperoni fan? Try cooked sausage, ham, bell peppers or just extra cheese.

Rusty Chef tip: Less sauce is more. Too much sauce makes the middle soggy, so use a thin layer.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat slices in a dry skillet over medium heat to bring back the crisp crust.
