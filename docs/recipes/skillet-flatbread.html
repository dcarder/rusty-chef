No-Yeast Skillet Flatbread

By Daniel Carder · Bread · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

No yeast, no oven, no waiting. Flour, yogurt and baking powder come together into a soft dough you can cook in a skillet in minutes.

Use them to scoop up curry, wrap tacos or brush with garlic butter as a side.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I figured these out when I ran out of bread and didn't want to make a trip to the store. Flour, yogurt and a hot skillet, and you've got warm bread in about twenty minutes. My kids like tearing them up to scoop curry or wrapping them around anything.– Daniel

Why yogurt?

The acid in yogurt reacts with baking powder to make the dough puff, and its fat keeps the bread soft. It replaces both the yeast and the rising time.

No-Yeast Skillet Flatbread

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon butter, melted (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
  2. Stir in the yogurt, then knead briefly into a soft dough. Add flour if sticky.
  3. Divide into 4 balls and roll each to about 1/4 inch thick.
  4. Cook in a hot, dry skillet for 1 to 2 minutes per side until puffed and spotted brown.
  5. Brush with melted butter if you like.

Pantry swaps

  • No yogurt? Use 3/4 cup milk plus 1 tablespoon lemon juice, left to sit for 5 minutes.
  • No baking powder? Use self-rising flour and skip the salt and baking powder.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep cooked flatbreads stacked under a clean towel. The trapped steam keeps them soft and bendable.

Storage

Keeps 2 days wrapped at room temperature, or freeze and reheat in a dry pan.
