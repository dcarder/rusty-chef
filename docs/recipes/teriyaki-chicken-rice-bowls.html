Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowls

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 25 minutes · Japanese & Asian

Jump to recipe

Bottled teriyaki sauce is mostly sugar. A homemade version takes 2 minutes with soy sauce, honey and garlic.

Served over rice with some vegetables, it makes a lunch bowl that holds up all week.

Thicken the sauce at the end

A little cornstarch mixed with water turns the thin soy and honey mixture into a glossy sauce that coats every piece of chicken.

Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowls

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$1.65/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken thighs or breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 3 tablespoons water
  • 3 cups cooked rice
  • 2 cups frozen vegetables, cooked (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Stir the soy sauce, honey, garlic, ginger and 1/4 cup of water together in a small bowl.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 6 to 8 minutes, until browned and cooked through.
  3. Pour in the sauce and bring to a simmer.
  4. Stir the cornstarch mixture, add it to the pan and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the sauce is thick and glossy.
  5. Serve over rice with the vegetables, topped with green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No honey? Use brown or white sugar.
  • No fresh garlic? Use 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder.

Rusty Chef tip: Chicken thighs stay juicy when reheated, so they're the better choice if you're packing lunches.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge.
