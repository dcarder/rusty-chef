Pantry Shakshuka

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Shakshuka is a North African and Middle Eastern dish of eggs cooked in a bubbling, spiced tomato sauce. It works for breakfast, lunch or dinner, and it all happens in one pan.

A can of tomatoes, an onion and a few spices are all you need. Serve it straight from the skillet with bread for dipping.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This is my favorite thing to make when it's just my wife and me. It feels fancy, like brunch at a restaurant, but it's eggs and a can of tomatoes. I serve it right in the skillet with bread for dipping, and there are barely any dishes.– Daniel

Don't stir once the eggs go in

Make little wells in the sauce with a spoon, crack the eggs in and cover the pan. The trapped steam sets the whites while the yolks stay runny. Stirring breaks the eggs and turns the dish into scrambled eggs in sauce.

Pantry Shakshuka

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$1.53/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 to 6 eggs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Crumbled feta or chopped parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until slightly thickened.
  4. Make wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover and cook on low for 5 to 8 minutes until the whites are set. Top with cheese or herbs and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No paprika? Use chili powder or a pinch of red pepper flakes.
  • No cumin? Skip it, or use a little curry powder.

Rusty Chef tip: Warm the bread in the oven while the eggs cook. Shakshuka is all about mopping up the sauce.

Storage

Best eaten right away. The sauce alone keeps 4 days in the fridge, so you can make it ahead and add fresh eggs.
