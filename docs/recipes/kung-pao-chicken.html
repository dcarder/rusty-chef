Easy Kung Pao Chicken

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Kung pao chicken is a Sichuan classic that became a takeout favorite: tender chicken, crunchy peanuts and a glossy sauce that's sweet, sour and a little spicy all at once.

It comes together in about 25 minutes with sauces you already have.

Choose your heat

Kung pao is meant to have a little kick. Start with 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes for a family-friendly version, or use a full teaspoon if you like it hot.

Easy Kung Pao Chicken

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.10/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce, divided
  • 2 tablespoons vinegar (rice vinegar is best)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar or honey
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger or 1 tablespoon fresh, grated
  • 1/2 to 1 teaspoon chili flakes
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
  • 3 green onions, sliced (optional)
  • Cooked rice, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken with the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon soy sauce.
  2. In a small bowl, stir together the remaining 2 tablespoons soy sauce, the vinegar, sugar and 2 tablespoons water.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add the chicken in one layer and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring now and then, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Add the bell pepper, garlic, ginger and chili flakes and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  5. Pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until it thickens and coats the chicken. Stir in the peanuts and green onions and serve over rice.

Pantry swaps

  • No peanuts? Use cashews, or leave them out.
  • No bell pepper? Use celery, zucchini or a cup of frozen stir-fry vegetables.

Rusty Chef tip: Stir in the peanuts at the very end so they stay crunchy.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet or microwave.
