Pantry Bean Chili

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 40 minutes

Jump to recipe

This chili uses only cans and spices, so you can make it any day of the week without shopping.

It's hearty, warming and gets better the next day.

From Daniel's kitchen

I make a big pot of this when I know the week is going to be crazy. It's all canned goods, so I can make it any time, and it tastes even better the next day. My kids pile on the cheese, and I freeze the leftovers for later.

Use a variety of beans

Mixing two or three kinds of beans gives the chili more interesting texture and flavor.

Pantry Bean Chili

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook30 min
  • Total40 min
  • Serves6

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 can chickpeas or pinto beans, drained (optional)
  • 1 cup stock or water
  • Salt
  • Shredded cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the onion in oil for 6 minutes. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes, beans and stock.
  3. Simmer uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
  4. Season with salt.
  5. Serve topped with cheese, sour cream or green onion.

Pantry swaps

  • Want meat? Brown 1 lb ground beef with the onion.
  • No chili powder? Paprika plus a pinch of cayenne.

Rusty Chef tip: A teaspoon of cocoa powder or a square of dark chocolate adds depth without tasting sweet.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.
