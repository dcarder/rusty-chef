Fast-Food Style Crispy Chicken Sandwich

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

The crispy chicken sandwich wars made these famous, but you can make a better one at home.

Soaking the chicken in pickle juice or buttermilk keeps it juicy and seasoned.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

When the chicken sandwich craze hit, my wife and kids wanted one every week. Four of them at the drive-thru adds up fast, so I learned to make them at home. The pickle juice soak is my secret, and honestly, they're juicier than any fast-food version we've tried.– Daniel

Flatten and soak the chicken

Pound the chicken to an even thickness so it cooks evenly, then soak it in pickle juice or milk for at least 15 minutes. That's what makes it juicy all the way through.

Fast-Food Style Crispy Chicken Sandwich

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.65/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts, halved and pounded thin
  • 1/2 cup pickle juice or milk
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • Oil for frying
  • 4 burger buns, buttered and toasted
  • Mayo and pickles

Instructions

  1. Soak the chicken in the pickle juice or milk for 15 minutes to 2 hours.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt. Whisk the egg with 2 tablespoons of the soaking liquid.
  3. Dip the chicken in the flour, then the egg, then the flour again, pressing to coat.
  4. Fry in 1/2 inch of oil at 350°F for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve on toasted buns with mayo and pickles.

Pantry swaps

  • Spicy version? Add 1 teaspoon cayenne to the flour and brush the cooked chicken with hot sauce.
  • Air fryer? Spray the coated chicken with oil and air fry at 380°F for 12 minutes, flipping halfway.

Rusty Chef tip: Drizzle a tablespoon of the egg mix into the flour and rub it in to make little clumps. They fry into extra-crunchy bits.

Storage

Best fresh. Reheat cooked chicken in the oven or air fryer to keep it crisp.
