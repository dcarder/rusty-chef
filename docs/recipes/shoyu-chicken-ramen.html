Easy Shoyu Chicken Ramen

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Shoyu means soy sauce in Japanese, and it's one of the classic ramen broths.

Skip the salty seasoning packet and build a real broth from stock, soy sauce, garlic and ginger in about 5 minutes.

The 6½-minute egg

Six and a half minutes in boiling water gives you set whites and a soft, jammy yolk, just like a ramen shop. Go 8 minutes if you like it firmer.

Easy Shoyu Chicken Ramen

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.80/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 packs instant ramen (seasoning packets not needed)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger or 1 teaspoon fresh, grated
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup spinach (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Gently lower in the eggs and cook for 6 1/2 minutes. Move them to cold water, then peel and cut in half.
  2. Meanwhile, simmer the stock, soy sauce, garlic, ginger and sugar in a medium pot for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the noodles and cook for 3 minutes, adding the chicken and spinach for the last minute.
  4. Stir in the sesame oil and divide between two bowls. Top with the eggs and green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No chicken? Use tofu, leftover pork or an extra egg.
  • Like it spicy? Add chili flakes or a spoonful of chili oil to each bowl.

Rusty Chef tip: Cook the noodles right in the broth so they soak up all that flavor.

Storage

Best eaten fresh, since the noodles soften. The broth alone keeps for 3 days in the fridge.
