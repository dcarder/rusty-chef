Weeknight Shepherd's Pie

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 60 minutes

Jump to recipe

Shepherd's pie is real comfort food, and it's built from cheap staples: ground meat, a bag of frozen vegetables and potatoes.

It makes enough for leftovers and freezes well.

Rough up the top

Drag a fork across the mashed potatoes before baking. The ridges catch the heat and turn crisp and golden.

Weeknight Shepherd's Pie

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook45 min
  • Total60 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$1.90/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 lb potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 4 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 lb ground beef or lamb
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced (optional)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 cup beef or chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup or tomato paste
  • 2 cups frozen vegetables
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15 minutes until tender. Drain, then mash with 3 tablespoons of the butter, the milk and a good pinch of salt.
  2. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Brown the beef with the onion and carrots in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 8 minutes. Drain off excess fat.
  3. Add the garlic and flour and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the stock and ketchup and simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.
  4. Stir in the frozen vegetables and season with salt and pepper. Spread in a baking dish.
  5. Spoon the mashed potatoes over the top, rough it up with a fork, dot with the remaining butter and sprinkle with cheese if you like. Bake for 20 minutes until golden.

Pantry swaps

  • No fresh potatoes? Use instant mashed potatoes made a little thicker than usual.
  • No stock? Use water with an extra teaspoon of soy sauce.

Rusty Chef tip: Let the filling cool for a few minutes before adding the potatoes so they sit on top instead of sinking in.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
