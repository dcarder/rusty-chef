Easy Skillet Chicken Tacos

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Chicken tacos are a weeknight favorite that cost far less than ordering out. A pound of chicken thighs, a few pantry spices and a pack of tortillas feed the whole family.

Set out the toppings and let everyone build their own. It's the easiest way to keep picky eaters happy.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Taco night is a must in our house, and these are the ones my wife and kids beg for. Chicken thighs are cheaper than breasts and way harder to mess up, which matters on the nights I'm running on empty. I set out cheese, salsa and sour cream, and everybody builds their own, so there's no fighting over toppings.– Daniel

Why thighs beat breasts for tacos

Boneless chicken thighs are usually cheaper than breasts and stay juicy even if you cook them a minute too long. Breasts dry out fast in a hot skillet. Slice the chicken after it rests for 5 minutes so the juices stay in the meat.

Easy Skillet Chicken Tacos

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.69/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Shredded cheese (optional)
  • Salsa (optional)
  • Sour cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, paprika and salt. Rub it all over the chicken.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5 to 7 minutes per side until well browned and cooked through.
  3. Move the chicken to a cutting board, squeeze the lime over it and rest for 5 minutes.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 30 seconds per side.
  5. Slice the chicken and fill the tortillas. Top with cheese, salsa and sour cream.

Pantry swaps

  • No chicken thighs? Use chicken breasts sliced in half lengthwise, and cook 4 to 5 minutes per side.
  • No lime? A splash of vinegar or orange juice adds the same brightness.
  • Leftover chicken? Shred it, warm it in a pan with the spices and a splash of water, and skip to step 4.

Rusty Chef tip: Char the tortillas directly over a gas burner for a few seconds per side. It makes them taste like they came from a taco truck.

Storage

Cooked chicken keeps 4 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet and use it for tacos, quesadillas or rice bowls.
