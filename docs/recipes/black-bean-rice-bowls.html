Southwest Black Bean Rice Bowls

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 20 minutes · Mexican & Tex-Mex

Jump to recipe

These burrito-style bowls take everything good about a burrito and skip the wrapping.

With leftover rice and a few cans they come together in 20 minutes and pack well for lunches.

Season the beans, not just the bowl

Simmering the beans and corn with cumin and chili powder for a few minutes lets the spices soak in, so every bite has flavor.

Southwest Black Bean Rice Bowls

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$1.18/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup canned or frozen corn
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt, to taste
  • Sour cream, to serve (optional)
  • Lime wedges, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the corn and cook for 3 minutes until lightly charred in spots.
  2. Add the black beans, cumin, chili powder, a pinch of salt and 3 tablespoons of water. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring now and then.
  3. Warm the rice.
  4. Divide the rice between bowls and top with the bean and corn mixture.
  5. Finish with salsa, cheese, and sour cream and lime if you have them.

Pantry swaps

  • No corn? Use diced bell pepper or frozen mixed vegetables.
  • Want more protein? Add leftover chicken or ground beef.

Rusty Chef tip: For meal prep, pack the salsa and sour cream separately so the rice doesn't go soggy.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Reheat the rice and beans, then add the cold toppings.
