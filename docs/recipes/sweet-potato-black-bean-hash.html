Sweet Potato & Black Bean Skillet

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Sweet potatoes and black beans are a classic pairing: sweet, smoky, filling and colorful.

This skillet works as a vegetarian dinner, a burrito filling or a breakfast hash topped with eggs.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Florida always has good sweet potatoes, and this is one of my favorite ways to use them. It's filling, colorful and costs very little. Put a fried egg on top and it works for breakfast, lunch or dinner.– Daniel

Cut small, even pieces

Half-inch cubes cook through and caramelize at the same time. Big chunks burn outside before softening inside.

Sweet Potato & Black Bean Skillet

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves3

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • Salt
  • Lime juice (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sweet potato and a pinch of salt.
  2. Cover and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Uncover, add the onion and spices and cook for 8 to 10 minutes until the sweet potato is tender and browned.
  4. Stir in the beans and cook for 2 minutes until hot.
  5. Season and finish with lime juice.

Pantry swaps

  • No sweet potatoes? Regular potatoes or butternut squash.
  • No chili powder? Smoked paprika.

Rusty Chef tip: Covering the pan at the start steams the sweet potatoes so they soften before browning.

Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
