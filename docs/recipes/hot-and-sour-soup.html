Easy Hot and Sour Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 20 minutes · Chinese

Jump to recipe

Hot and sour soup is a Chinese restaurant favorite, and the base is just stock, soy sauce and vinegar.

It's on the table in 20 minutes for about the price of a cup of coffee.

Taste and adjust at the end

The "hot" in this soup traditionally comes from pepper, and the "sour" from vinegar. Taste before serving and add more of either until it's as tangy and peppery as you like.

Easy Hot and Sour Soup

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.30/serving

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken stock
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons vinegar (rice vinegar is best)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger or 1 tablespoon fresh, grated
  • 1/2 teaspoon white or black pepper, plus salt to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • 7 oz firm tofu, cut into small cubes (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons cornstarch mixed with 3 tablespoons cold water
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • 3 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Bring the stock, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, ginger, pepper and chili flakes to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Add the tofu, if using, and simmer for 3 minutes.
  3. Stir the cornstarch mixture again, then pour it in while stirring. Simmer for 1 minute until the soup thickens slightly.
  4. Turn the heat to low. Slowly drizzle in the beaten eggs while gently stirring in one direction to make thin ribbons.
  5. Turn off the heat and stir in the sesame oil. Taste and add more vinegar or pepper if you like. Top with green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • Have mushrooms? Add a cup of sliced mushrooms with the tofu.
  • No tofu? Use shredded cooked chicken or pork, or leave it out.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep the heat low and pour the eggs in a thin stream. That's how you get silky ribbons instead of scrambled chunks.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat gently. It doesn't freeze well because of the egg and tofu.
