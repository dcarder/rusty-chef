Peanut Butter Banana Oatmeal

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

A ripe banana does two jobs in this oatmeal: it sweetens the oats and makes them extra creamy.

Stir in peanut butter at the end and you get a warm, filling breakfast with no added sugar needed.

Use the spottiest banana

The browner the banana, the sweeter it is. Bananas with lots of brown spots are perfect here and much harder to enjoy any other way.

Peanut Butter Banana Oatmeal

  • Prep2 min
  • Cook8 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$0.57/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 teaspoon honey, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mash half the banana in a small saucepan. Slice the other half and set aside.
  2. Add the oats, milk, water, cinnamon and salt. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.
  3. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  4. Take off the heat and stir in 1 tablespoon of the peanut butter.
  5. Divide between bowls and top with the banana slices, the rest of the peanut butter and a drizzle of honey if you like.

Pantry swaps

  • No milk? Use all water. It will be a little less creamy.
  • No peanut butter? Any nut or seed butter, or a spoonful of cocoa powder.

Rusty Chef tip: Oatmeal keeps thickening as it sits. Add a splash of milk just before serving to loosen it.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat with a splash of milk.
