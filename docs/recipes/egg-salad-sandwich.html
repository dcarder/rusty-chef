Classic Egg Salad Sandwich

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 22 minutes

Jump to recipe

Egg salad is a cheap, filling lunch made from ingredients most people always have.

The key is perfectly cooked eggs with tender whites and creamy, not chalky, yolks.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Egg salad is one of my favorite cheap lunches. A dozen eggs goes a long way, and it's an easy way to use up hard-boiled eggs after the holidays. When it's just me, I keep a batch in the fridge for quick lunches during the week.– Daniel

Perfect hard-boiled eggs

Place eggs in boiling water, cook for 11 minutes, then plunge them into ice water. This prevents the gray ring around the yolk and makes them easier to peel.

Classic Egg Salad Sandwich

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook12 min
  • Total22 min
  • Serves3
  • Est. cost$1.04/serving

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 green onion, sliced (optional)
  • 6 slices bread

Instructions

  1. Gently lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 11 minutes.
  2. Transfer to ice water for 5 minutes, then peel.
  3. Chop the eggs and mix with the mayo, mustard, green onion, salt and pepper.
  4. Spread onto bread and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No mayo? Plain yogurt or mashed avocado.
  • Want a kick? Add a pinch of paprika or curry powder.

Rusty Chef tip: Older eggs peel more easily than very fresh ones.

Storage

Egg salad keeps 3 days in the fridge.
