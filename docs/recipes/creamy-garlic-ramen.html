Creamy Garlic Butter Ramen

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 15 minutes · Japanese & Asian

Jump to recipe

This viral-style ramen turns a cheap pack of noodles into something that eats like a creamy carbonara.

It takes 10 minutes and a few fridge basics.

Keep the heat low

The egg makes the sauce silky, but high heat will scramble it. Keep the burner on low and keep tossing until it turns creamy.

Creamy Garlic Butter Ramen

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$0.85/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 packs instant ramen
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles in boiling water for 2 minutes, until just tender. Save 1/2 cup of the water, then drain.
  2. In the same pot, melt the butter over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook for 1 minute until fragrant, not brown.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the milk, egg, parmesan, soy sauce and half of one seasoning packet, if you like.
  4. Turn the heat to low. Add the noodles, pour in the egg mixture and toss constantly for 1 to 2 minutes until the sauce is creamy and coats the noodles. Add splashes of noodle water if it gets too thick.
  5. Top with green onions and more chili flakes.

Pantry swaps

  • No parmesan? Use a slice or two of American or a handful of shredded cheddar.
  • Skip the egg? Use 3/4 cup milk instead. It's a little thinner but still creamy.

Rusty Chef tip: Use only part of a seasoning packet, or none at all. The cheese and soy sauce already add plenty of salt.

Storage

Best eaten right away.
