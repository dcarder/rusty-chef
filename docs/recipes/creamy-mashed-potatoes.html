Creamy Mashed Potatoes

By Daniel Carder · Side · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Good mashed potatoes only need a few things: the right potatoes, enough butter, and warm milk.

They go with almost any dinner, and leftovers make great potato cakes.

Warm the milk

Cold milk cools the potatoes and makes them gluey. Warm the milk and butter together first so the mash stays fluffy and hot.

Creamy Mashed Potatoes

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$0.60/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 lb potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled (optional)
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 2 green onions or chives, sliced (optional)
  • 1/4 cup sour cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. Put the potatoes and garlic in a pot, cover with cold water and add 1 teaspoon of salt. Bring to a boil.
  2. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until a fork slides easily into the potatoes. Drain well and return to the pot.
  3. Meanwhile, warm the milk and butter in a small pan or the microwave.
  4. Mash the potatoes and garlic, then stir in the warm milk mixture, sour cream if using, and the remaining salt.
  5. Top with green onions or chives and an extra pat of butter.

Pantry swaps

  • No milk? Use some of the potato cooking water, or stock.
  • Want them extra rich? Use cream instead of milk and add more butter.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't use a blender or food processor. It overworks the potatoes and turns them gluey. A masher or fork works best.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Reheat with a splash of milk.
