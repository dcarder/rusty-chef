Easy Black Bean Burgers

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Black bean burgers cost a fraction of beef burgers and are packed with fiber and protein.

The secret is drying the beans so the patties hold together.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I started making these when ground beef prices got out of hand. One can of beans makes four burgers, and with cheese, ketchup and pickles, my kids eat them without a fuss. Drying the beans first was the game changer; my first batch fell apart all over the pan.– Daniel

Dry the beans

Wet beans make mushy burgers that fall apart. Pat the drained beans dry with a towel before mashing, and chill the shaped patties for 15 minutes if you have time.

Easy Black Bean Burgers

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 can black beans, drained and patted dry
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs or oats
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 4 buns

Instructions

  1. Mash the beans with a fork, leaving some chunks.
  2. Mix in the breadcrumbs, egg, onion, spices and a pinch of salt.
  3. Shape into 4 patties.
  4. Fry in oil over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes per side until crispy.
  5. Serve on buns with your favorite toppings.

Pantry swaps

  • No egg? Use 2 tablespoons mayo to bind them.
  • Bake instead? Bake at 375°F for 20 minutes, flipping halfway.

Rusty Chef tip: Melt a slice of cheese on top for the last minute in the pan.

Storage

Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Freeze uncooked patties for up to 3 months.
