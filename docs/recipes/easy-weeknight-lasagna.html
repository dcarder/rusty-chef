Easy Weeknight Lasagna

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 65 minutes

Jump to recipe

Lasagna feeds a crowd and tastes even better the next day.

This version keeps things simple: no-boil noodles, a quick meat sauce, and a cream cheese layer instead of ricotta.

Let it rest

Give the lasagna 15 minutes out of the oven before cutting. The layers firm up so you get neat slices instead of a sloppy pile.

Easy Weeknight Lasagna

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook45 min
  • Total65 min
  • Serves8
  • Est. cost$1.69/serving

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles (no-boil or regular, cooked)
  • 1 lb ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (14 oz) tomato sauce
  • 2 teaspoons dried Italian herbs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Brown the beef and onion in a large pot for 8 minutes, then drain the fat. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, herbs, salt and 1/2 cup of water. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Mix the cream cheese, egg, half the parmesan and 1 cup of the mozzarella until smooth.
  4. Spread 1 cup of sauce in a 9x13-inch dish. Layer 4 noodles, a third of the cream cheese mix, a quarter of the sauce and 1/2 cup of mozzarella. Repeat twice, then finish with noodles, the rest of the sauce, mozzarella and parmesan.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for 15 minutes more until bubbling and golden. Rest for 15 minutes before slicing.

Pantry swaps

  • Have ricotta? Use 15 oz ricotta in place of the cream cheese.
  • Vegetarian? Skip the meat and add a bag of chopped spinach and a can of lentils to the sauce.

Rusty Chef tip: Make sure every noodle is covered with sauce, especially at the edges. Dry noodles stay hard.

Storage

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer, whole or in slices.
