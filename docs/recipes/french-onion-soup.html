Easy French Onion Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 70 minutes

Jump to recipe

French onion soup turns a bag of cheap onions into something special. It just takes time.

The onions cook slowly until deep brown and sweet, then simmer in broth under a lid of cheesy toast.

Don't rush the onions

Real caramelized onions take 40 to 45 minutes over medium-low heat. That slow cooking is where all the flavor comes from, so don't turn up the heat to hurry them.

Easy French Onion Soup

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook60 min
  • Total70 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.70/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 6 cups beef stock
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme or Italian herbs
  • 4 thick slices of bread, toasted
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Swiss, provolone or mozzarella

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onions, sugar and salt and stir to coat.
  2. Lower the heat to medium-low and cook for 40 to 45 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until the onions are deep golden brown. Add a splash of water if they start to stick.
  3. Stir in the garlic and flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the stock and thyme, scraping up the brown bits. Simmer for 15 minutes. Taste and add salt if needed.
  5. Heat the broiler. Ladle the soup into oven-safe bowls, top each with toast and cheese, and broil for 2 to 3 minutes until bubbling and golden.

Pantry swaps

  • No oven-safe bowls? Melt the cheese on the toast under the broiler, then float it on top of the soup.
  • No beef stock? Use chicken or vegetable stock with a splash of soy sauce for depth.

Rusty Chef tip: Slice the onions from root to tip rather than across. The strips hold their shape better during the long cook.

Storage

The soup without toppings keeps for 5 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
