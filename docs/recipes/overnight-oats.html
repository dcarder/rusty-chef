Basic Overnight Oats

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 5 minutes

Jump to recipe

Overnight oats are the easiest breakfast you'll ever make. Stir oats and milk together in a jar, leave it in the fridge, and breakfast is waiting in the morning.

The basic ratio is endlessly adaptable: add peanut butter, cocoa, jam, frozen berries or sliced banana.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

On mornings when I'm running behind and getting everybody out the door, these jars save me. I make five on Sunday night and breakfast is handled for the week. My wife and kids pick their own toppings, which means less arguing at 7 a.m.– Daniel

Use rolled oats, not instant

Rolled (old-fashioned) oats soften overnight but keep a pleasant chew. Instant oats turn to mush, and steel-cut oats stay too hard without cooking.

Basic Overnight Oats

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total5 min
  • Serves1
  • Est. cost$0.35/serving

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
  • Pinch of cinnamon (optional)
  • Sliced banana or berries (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add the oats, milk, yogurt, honey and cinnamon to a jar or container.
  2. Stir well, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  3. In the morning, stir again and add a splash of milk if it's too thick.
  4. Top with fruit and eat cold, or microwave for 1 minute if you prefer it warm.

Pantry swaps

  • No yogurt? Add an extra 1/4 cup milk.
  • Dairy-free? Use any plant milk and a spoonful of nut butter for creaminess.

Rusty Chef tip: Make five jars on Sunday night and you've got breakfast sorted for the whole work week.

Storage

Keeps up to 4 days in the fridge. Add fresh fruit on the day you eat it.
