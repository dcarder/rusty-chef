Irish Boxty Potato Pancakes

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 35 minutes · Irish

Jump to recipe

Boxty is a traditional Irish potato pancake, crispy on the outside and soft and tender inside.

It's a great way to use up leftover mashed potatoes, and it's delicious with eggs, bacon or a spoonful of sour cream.

Squeeze the potatoes dry

Wrap the grated potato in a clean kitchen towel and twist out as much liquid as you can. Dry potatoes fry up crisp instead of soggy.

Irish Boxty Potato Pancakes

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$0.50/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 large potato, peeled and grated
  • 1 cup leftover mashed potatoes
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons butter or oil, for frying
  • Sour cream, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Squeeze the grated potato dry in a clean towel.
  2. In a bowl, mix the grated potato, mashed potatoes, flour, baking powder, salt and green onions.
  3. Whisk the milk and egg together and stir them in to make a thick batter.
  4. Heat 1 tablespoon butter in a skillet over medium heat. Scoop 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and flatten to about 1/2 inch thick.
  5. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes per side until golden and crisp, adding more butter as needed.
  6. Serve hot with sour cream, eggs or bacon.

Pantry swaps

  • No leftover mashed potatoes? Use 2 grated potatoes instead and add 1/4 cup more flour.
  • Make it a meal? Fold in a handful of chopped ham or shredded cheese.

Rusty Chef tip: Keep the heat at medium. Too hot and the outside burns before the center cooks through.

Storage

Keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet or toaster oven to keep them crisp.
