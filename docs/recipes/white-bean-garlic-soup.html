Tuscan White Bean & Garlic Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

Inspired by Tuscan peasant cooking, this soup turns a couple of cans of white beans into something rich and elegant.

Serve it with crusty bread and a drizzle of good olive oil on top.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This feels like something you'd order at a nice Italian restaurant, but it's two cans of beans and a lot of garlic. When it's just me, I make a pot and eat it for a few days with crusty bread. It never gets old.– Daniel

Mash some of the beans

Pressing a cup or so of beans against the side of the pot thickens the broth into something silky and creamy.

Tuscan White Bean & Garlic Soup

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.52/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) white beans, drained
  • 4 cups stock
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Handful of spinach (optional)
  • Grated parmesan (optional)

Instructions

  1. Warm the oil and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 2 minutes until golden.
  2. Add the beans, stock and herbs. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Mash about a third of the beans to thicken the soup.
  4. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  5. Season and serve with parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil.

Pantry swaps

  • No white beans? Chickpeas work well.
  • No spinach? Use kale, cabbage or frozen peas.

Rusty Chef tip: Drop a parmesan rind into the pot while it simmers for a deeper, savory flavor.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in the fridge.
