10-Minute Egg Drop Soup

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

Egg drop soup is one of the quickest soups there is: hot broth, a little cornstarch and beaten eggs swirled in to form delicate ribbons.

It's light, comforting and perfect when you're feeling under the weather.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

When one of the kids or my wife is home sick, this is what I make. It's gentle, warm and ready in ten minutes. It costs pennies compared to ordering it, and they say mine is better than the restaurant's, which I'll take.– Daniel

Pour slowly while stirring

Drizzle the beaten egg in a thin stream while gently stirring the broth in one direction. Slow pouring makes fine, silky ribbons; dumping it in makes clumps.

10-Minute Egg Drop Soup

  • Prep2 min
  • Cook8 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.55/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • Sliced green onion (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring the stock to a simmer with the soy sauce.
  2. Mix the cornstarch with 2 tablespoons cold water and stir it into the stock. Simmer for 1 minute until slightly thickened.
  3. Stir the broth in a slow circle and drizzle in the egg in a thin stream.
  4. Turn off the heat after 30 seconds.
  5. Add sesame oil and green onion and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No cornstarch? Skip it. The soup will be thinner but still delicious.
  • Want more? Add frozen peas or corn with the stock.

Rusty Chef tip: A pinch of white pepper gives it that classic restaurant flavor.

Storage

Best eaten right away. Leftovers keep 2 days in the fridge.
