Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Sheet pan fajitas give you sizzling restaurant flavor with almost no work. Everything roasts together on one pan.

Set out tortillas and toppings and let everyone build their own.

Use a hot oven

A very hot oven gives the peppers and onions those charred edges you get from a fajita skillet. Spread everything out so it roasts instead of steaming.

Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.10/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breasts or thighs, sliced into strips
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 8 small tortillas, warmed
  • Lime wedges (optional)
  • Sour cream, salsa and cheese, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss the chicken, peppers and onion with the oil, spices and salt on a large baking sheet. Spread into a single layer.
  3. Roast for 18 to 22 minutes, stirring once halfway, until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are charred at the edges.
  4. Squeeze over some lime juice.
  5. Pile into warm tortillas and top with sour cream, salsa and cheese.

Pantry swaps

  • No chicken? Use sliced steak, shrimp or a can of black beans added for the last 5 minutes.
  • No fajita spices? Use 1 tablespoon of taco seasoning.

Rusty Chef tip: Warm the tortillas wrapped in foil in the oven for the last 5 minutes so they're soft and pliable.

Storage

The filling keeps for 4 days in the fridge.
