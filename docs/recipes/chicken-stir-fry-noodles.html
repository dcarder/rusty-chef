Chicken Stir-Fry Noodles

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 25 minutes

Jump to recipe

This is a takeout-style noodle stir-fry made with chicken, frozen vegetables and pantry noodles.

The sauce is a simple mix of soy sauce, honey, garlic and ginger, thickened so it coats everything.

Cook in stages

Cook the chicken first and take it out, then the vegetables. Adding everything at once crowds the pan and makes the chicken steam instead of brown.

Chicken Stir-Fry Noodles

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total25 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.73/serving

Ingredients

  • 8 oz spaghetti or ramen noodles
  • 1 lb chicken breast or thighs, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 3 cups frozen stir-fry vegetables
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles until just tender, then drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, ginger, cornstarch, sesame oil and 1/3 cup of water together.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet over high heat. Cook the chicken for 5 to 6 minutes until browned and cooked through, then set aside.
  4. Add the rest of the oil and the vegetables. Stir-fry for 4 minutes, then add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  5. Return the chicken, add the noodles and sauce, and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until glossy. Top with green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No chicken? Use tofu, shrimp, beef or scrambled eggs.
  • No honey? Use brown sugar.

Rusty Chef tip: Slice the chicken while it's partly frozen. It's much easier to cut into thin, even strips.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a hot pan with a splash of water.
