Smooth Homemade Hummus

By Daniel Carder · Snack · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

Homemade hummus is cheaper and tastier than store-bought, and it takes 10 minutes with a blender or food processor.

Serve it with vegetables, pita or crackers, or spread it in sandwiches.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Store-bought hummus is pricey for what it is, so I started making my own. One can of chickpeas makes a big bowl. My wife and kids eat it with carrots and crackers, and it's a snack I don't have to feel bad about.– Daniel

The ice water trick

Adding a few tablespoons of ice-cold water while blending makes hummus light and fluffy.

Smooth Homemade Hummus

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$0.33/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained (save the liquid)
  • 1/4 cup tahini (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Blend the lemon juice, garlic and salt and let it sit for 2 minutes to mellow the garlic.
  2. Add the tahini and olive oil and blend until creamy.
  3. Add the chickpeas and cumin and blend for 2 minutes.
  4. With the blender running, add 2 to 4 tablespoons ice water or chickpea liquid until smooth.
  5. Spread in a bowl, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with paprika.

Pantry swaps

  • No tahini? Use 2 tablespoons peanut butter or extra olive oil.
  • No lemon? White wine vinegar.

Rusty Chef tip: Blend longer than you think. An extra minute or two makes it noticeably smoother.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in the fridge.
