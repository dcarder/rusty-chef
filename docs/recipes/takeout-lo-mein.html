Better-Than-Takeout Lo Mein

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 20 minutes · Chinese

Jump to recipe

Lo mein is one of the most-ordered takeout dishes, and it's really just noodles, vegetables and a simple soy sauce mix.

Plain spaghetti stands in for lo mein noodles, and the sauce uses pantry staples you probably already have.

The sauce is the secret

Takeout lo mein gets its flavor from a sweet, salty sauce. Soy sauce, a little sugar and sesame oil get you very close, and a splash of the starchy pasta water helps it cling to every noodle.

Better-Than-Takeout Lo Mein

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$0.78/serving

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or lo mein noodles
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sugar or honey
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups frozen stir-fry or mixed vegetables
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, sliced (optional)
  • 3 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles in boiling water until just tender. Save 1/4 cup of the cooking water, then drain and rinse briefly with cold water so they don't stick.
  2. Stir the soy sauce, sugar and sesame oil together in a small bowl.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until hot and lightly browned.
  4. Add the garlic and the chicken, if using, and stir-fry for 1 minute.
  5. Add the noodles, sauce and a splash of the pasta water. Toss with tongs for 1 to 2 minutes until the noodles are glossy and hot. Top with green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • No sesame oil? Leave it out. It adds a toasty flavor but isn't essential.
  • Want more protein? Add scrambled eggs, leftover pork or shrimp.

Rusty Chef tip: Get the pan really hot before the vegetables go in. High heat gives you that slightly smoky takeout flavor instead of steamed vegetables.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a hot pan with a splash of water.
