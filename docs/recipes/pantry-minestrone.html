Pantry Minestrone

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

Minestrone is less a recipe than a way of using up whatever you have. Beans, pasta, tomatoes and any vegetables go into one pot.

This version leans on frozen vegetables and canned goods, so you can make it any time.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This is my clean-out-the-kitchen soup. Whatever half-used vegetables, beans and pasta are hanging around, they go in the pot. My kids like finding the little pasta shapes, and I like that nothing goes to waste.– Daniel

Cook the pasta in the soup

Adding small pasta directly to the pot thickens the broth with starch. Just add it near the end so it doesn't go mushy.

Pantry Minestrone

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves6

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups stock
  • 1 can (15 oz) white or kidney beans, drained
  • 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  • 3/4 cup small pasta
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Grated parmesan (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes, stock, herbs and beans. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the frozen vegetables and pasta. Simmer for 10 minutes until the pasta is tender.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve with parmesan on top.

Pantry swaps

  • No small pasta? Break spaghetti into short pieces.
  • No dried herbs? Use a bay leaf or a pinch of oregano.

Rusty Chef tip: If you plan to have leftovers, cook the pasta separately and add it to each bowl so it doesn't soak up all the broth.

Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
