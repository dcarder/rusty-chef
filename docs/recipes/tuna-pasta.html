Lemony Tuna Pasta

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 17 minutes

Jump to recipe

Canned tuna and pasta are a classic pantry partnership in Italian home cooking. This version is light, bright and ready while the pasta boils.

Oil-packed tuna gives the richest flavor, but water-packed works too.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This is my emergency dinner when I haven't planned anything and the cupboard is looking bare. Everybody usually has a can of tuna and a box of pasta. Fifteen minutes later, dinner's on the table and I didn't have to spend a dime extra.– Daniel

Add the tuna off the heat

Tuna is already cooked. Stirring it in at the end keeps it tender and flaky instead of dry.

Lemony Tuna Pasta

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook12 min
  • Total17 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.68/serving

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 1 can (5 oz) tuna, drained
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/4 teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon capers (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water. Save 1/2 cup of water before draining.
  2. Warm the oil, garlic and chili flakes in a skillet over low heat for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the pasta, capers and a splash of pasta water. Toss to coat.
  4. Turn off the heat and fold in the tuna and lemon juice.
  5. Season and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No lemon? A splash of vinegar.
  • No capers? Chopped olives or pickles.

Rusty Chef tip: Toast some breadcrumbs in olive oil and scatter them on top for crunch.

Storage

Keeps 2 days in the fridge. Good cold as a pasta salad.
