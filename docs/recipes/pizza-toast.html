Pizza Toast

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

Pizza toast is a quick, cheap lunch that kids can help make themselves.

Use any bread you have and whatever toppings are in the fridge.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

When my wife and kids are craving pizza and I'm not about to pay for delivery, this is my answer. Bread, a little sauce and cheese, and they get to pick their own toppings. It's ready in ten minutes, it costs pennies, and it makes them feel like they made lunch themselves.– Daniel

Toast it first

Lightly toasting the bread before adding the sauce keeps it crisp instead of soggy.

Pizza Toast

  • Prep3 min
  • Cook7 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$1.15/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 slices bread
  • 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes or pizza sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • Pepperoni or other toppings (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven or toaster oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toast the bread lightly.
  3. Spread with the sauce and sprinkle with herbs.
  4. Add the cheese and toppings.
  5. Bake for 5 to 7 minutes until bubbly.

Pantry swaps

  • No oven? Cook in a covered skillet over low heat until the cheese melts.
  • Garlic bread style? Butter the bread and sprinkle with garlic powder before toasting.

Rusty Chef tip: Thick-cut bread, English muffins or bagels make a sturdier base.
