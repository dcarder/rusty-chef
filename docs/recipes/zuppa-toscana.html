Restaurant-Style Zuppa Toscana

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

This creamy soup of sausage, potatoes and greens is one of the most-ordered soups at Italian chain restaurants.

At home, a whole pot costs about what one bowl does when you eat out.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

We used to order this soup every time we went to the Italian chain place, and one night I added up what we spent on soup alone. Now I make a whole pot for about the price of one bowl there. My wife loves it, and the kids pick out the greens at first but always come back for seconds of the sausage and potatoes.– Daniel

Slice the potatoes thin

Thin half-moon slices cook quickly and give that restaurant texture. Big chunks take longer and make the soup feel more like stew.

Restaurant-Style Zuppa Toscana

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$2.12/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground Italian sausage
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 6 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup milk or half-and-half
  • 2 big handfuls kale or spinach
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Brown the sausage in a large pot for 6 to 8 minutes. Drain most of the fat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then the garlic and chili flakes for 1 minute.
  3. Add the potatoes and stock. Simmer for 15 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
  4. Stir in the milk and greens and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the greens wilt.
  5. Season with salt and pepper and serve with bread.

Pantry swaps

  • No Italian sausage? Use any ground sausage plus 1/2 teaspoon fennel seed or dried Italian herbs.
  • Want it richer? Use heavy cream instead of milk.
  • Add bacon? Crumble a few cooked slices on top like the restaurant does.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't let the soup boil hard after adding the milk, or it can separate.

Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge. Reheat gently.
