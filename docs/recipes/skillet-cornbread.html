Skillet Cornbread

By Daniel Carder · Bread · 32 minutes

Jump to recipe

Cornbread baked in a hot skillet gets crisp, buttery edges and a tender middle.

It's the perfect partner for chili, soup or beans.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

You can't live in the South without a good cornbread, and a hot skillet is the secret. I make it every time we have chili or beans. My wife and kids like it a little sweet with honey butter, and it's gone before dinner's over.– Daniel

Preheat the pan

Pouring the batter into a sizzling hot, buttered skillet starts the crust immediately for crunchy edges.

Skillet Cornbread

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook22 min
  • Total32 min
  • Serves8
  • Est. cost$0.23/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted

Instructions

  1. Put a 10-inch cast-iron skillet in the oven and heat to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  3. Whisk the milk, egg and most of the melted butter, then stir into the dry ingredients until just combined.
  4. Carefully remove the hot skillet, add the remaining butter and swirl. Pour in the batter.
  5. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.

Pantry swaps

  • No skillet? Use an 8x8-inch baking pan and bake 20 to 25 minutes.
  • Less sweet? Use 1 tablespoon sugar.

Rusty Chef tip: Add a can of drained corn or some shredded cheddar for a heartier cornbread.

Storage

Keeps 3 days wrapped at room temperature.
