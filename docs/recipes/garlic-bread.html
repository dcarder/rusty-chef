Easy Garlic Bread

By Daniel Carder · Side · 15 minutes

Jump to recipe

Homemade garlic bread beats the frozen kind every time, and it's a great way to revive bread that's a day or two old.

Serve it with pasta, soup or baked ravioli.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

No spaghetti night in our house is complete without this. It's also my secret for making a cheap dinner feel special. Day-old bread works even better, so it's a great way to use up a loaf before it goes bad.– Daniel

Soften the butter first

Room-temperature butter mixes evenly with the garlic and spreads without tearing the bread.

Easy Garlic Bread

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves6

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf bread or baguette
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley or dried herbs (optional)
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Mix the butter, garlic, herbs and salt.
  3. Slice the bread in half lengthwise and spread with garlic butter. Sprinkle with parmesan.
  4. Bake on a sheet for 8 to 10 minutes until golden and crisp.
  5. Slice and serve hot.

Pantry swaps

  • No fresh garlic? 1 teaspoon garlic powder.
  • Want it cheesy? Add mozzarella before baking.

Rusty Chef tip: Broil for the last minute for extra-crisp, golden edges. Watch closely.

Storage

Garlic butter keeps 2 weeks in the fridge.
