Irish Colcannon

By Daniel Carder · Side · 35 minutes · Irish

Jump to recipe

Colcannon is Ireland's favorite way to dress up mashed potatoes: creamy, buttery and full of tender cabbage and green onions.

It's the classic side for Irish stew, sausages or ham, and it's made from some of the cheapest vegetables in the store.

Make a butter well

In Ireland, colcannon is served with a little well pressed into the middle and filled with melting butter. Dip each forkful into it on the way to your mouth.

Irish Colcannon

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves6
  • Est. cost$0.70/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lbs potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 6 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 3 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 3/4 cup milk, warmed

Instructions

  1. Put the potatoes in a pot, cover with cold salted water and bring to a boil. Simmer for about 15 minutes until very tender.
  2. Meanwhile, melt 2 tablespoons butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the cabbage and a pinch of salt and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until tender. Stir in the green onions for the last minute.
  3. Drain the potatoes and return them to the pot. Mash with 2 tablespoons butter and the warm milk until creamy.
  4. Fold in the cabbage and green onions. Season well with salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon into a bowl, make a well in the center and add the remaining butter to melt.

Pantry swaps

  • No cabbage? Use chopped kale or spinach.
  • Want it heartier? Stir in crumbled cooked bacon.

Rusty Chef tip: Warm the milk before adding it. Cold milk cools the potatoes down and makes them gluey.

Storage

Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Shape leftovers into patties and fry them in butter until crispy.
