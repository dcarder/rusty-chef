Crispy Smashed Potatoes

By Daniel Carder · Side · 50 minutes

Jump to recipe

Smashed potatoes give you the best of both worlds: fluffy like a baked potato inside, crispy like a fry outside.

They're boiled, flattened with a glass, then roasted in a hot oven until golden.

Let them steam dry

After boiling, let the potatoes sit in the colander for a few minutes so the steam escapes. Drier potatoes get much crispier in the oven.

Crispy Smashed Potatoes

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook45 min
  • Total50 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$0.60/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb small potatoes
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons grated parmesan (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes until tender. Drain and let them steam dry for 5 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C) and drizzle a baking sheet with half the olive oil.
  3. Spread the potatoes out on the sheet and press each one flat with the bottom of a glass until about 1/2 inch thick.
  4. Drizzle with the rest of the oil and sprinkle with salt, garlic powder and paprika.
  5. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes until deep golden and crispy. Sprinkle with parmesan and parsley and serve hot.

Pantry swaps

  • Only big potatoes? Cut them into 2-inch chunks before boiling.
  • No olive oil? Use any oil or melted butter.

Rusty Chef tip: Don't flip them. Leave them alone in the oven so the bottoms get really crisp.

Storage

Best fresh. Leftovers reheat well in a hot oven or air fryer.
