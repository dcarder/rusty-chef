Classic Ground Beef Tacos

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

Taco night is a family favorite for a reason: it's fast, cheap and everyone gets to build their own.

This homemade seasoning beats the packet, and you probably have every spice already.

Make your own seasoning

Chili powder, cumin, garlic and paprika simmered with a splash of water and a little tomato make a saucy filling with more flavor than a seasoning packet.

Classic Ground Beef Tacos

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.05/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup or tomato sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 8 small tortillas or taco shells
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Salsa, to serve (optional)
  • Sour cream, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef and onion in a skillet over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking up the meat. Drain off most of the fat.
  2. Add the garlic, chili powder, cumin and paprika and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the ketchup, salt and 1/3 cup of water. Simmer for 3 to 5 minutes until saucy.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or the microwave.
  5. Fill the tortillas with the beef and top with cheese, salsa and sour cream.

Pantry swaps

  • No ground beef? Use ground turkey, shredded chicken or mashed black beans.
  • Want crunchy tacos? Use hard taco shells warmed in the oven.

Rusty Chef tip: Stretch the meat further by stirring in a can of drained black beans with the water.

Storage

The taco meat keeps for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
