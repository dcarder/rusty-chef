Pizzeria Garlic Knots

By Daniel Carder · Bread · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

Garlic knots are the side everyone orders with pizza, and a dozen at home costs less than a few at a pizzeria.

Use our homemade pizza dough or a store-bought dough ball.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Every pizza order in our house used to come with a side of garlic knots, and they're one of the most overpriced things on the menu. Now I tie a dozen from one ball of dough for pocket change. My wife and kids fight over the last one every single time.– Daniel

Brush them twice

Brush the knots with garlic butter before baking and again right out of the oven. The second coat soaks in and makes them taste like the pizzeria's.

Pizzeria Garlic Knots

  • Prep20 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves12
  • Est. cost$0.37/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough (homemade or store-bought)
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • Pinch of salt
  • Marinara for dipping (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet.
  2. Cut the dough into 12 pieces, roll each into a 6-inch rope and tie in a loose knot.
  3. Mix the melted butter, garlic, herbs and salt. Brush half over the knots.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until golden.
  5. Brush with the remaining garlic butter, toss with parmesan and serve warm with marinara.

Pantry swaps

  • No pizza dough? Use canned biscuit dough rolled into ropes; bake 10 to 12 minutes.
  • No fresh garlic? Use 1 teaspoon garlic powder in the butter.

Rusty Chef tip: Let the dough sit at room temperature for 15 minutes before shaping so it stretches without springing back.

Storage

Best warm. Keeps 2 days; reheat in the oven for 5 minutes.
