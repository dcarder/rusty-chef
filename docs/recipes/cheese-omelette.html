Fluffy Cheese Omelette

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 8 minutes

Jump to recipe

A good omelette takes 5 minutes and three ingredients, and it's one of the fastest hot breakfasts you can make.

Once you've got the basic cheese omelette down, you can fill it with whatever's left in the fridge.

Low and slow

Medium-low heat is the secret to a tender, golden omelette. High heat makes the eggs tough and rubbery before the middle has set.

Fluffy Cheese Omelette

  • Prep3 min
  • Cook5 min
  • Total8 min
  • Serves1
  • Est. cost$1.30/serving

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon milk (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/3 cup shredded cheese
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 green onion, sliced (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons diced ham (optional)
  • A handful of spinach (optional)

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs, milk and salt in a bowl until no streaks of white remain.
  2. Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat until it foams.
  3. Pour in the eggs. As the edges set, gently push them toward the middle with a spatula and tilt the pan so the runny egg fills the gaps.
  4. When the eggs are almost set but still slightly wet on top, about 2 minutes, scatter the cheese and any fillings over one half.
  5. Fold the omelette in half, cook for 30 seconds more so the cheese melts, then slide it onto a plate.

Pantry swaps

  • No cheese? Fill it with sautéed vegetables, salsa or leftover meat instead.
  • No nonstick pan? Use a little extra butter and keep the heat low.

Rusty Chef tip: Take the omelette off the heat while the top still looks a little shiny. It keeps cooking from its own heat.

Storage

Best eaten right away.
