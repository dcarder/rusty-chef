Creamy Stovetop Rice Pudding

By Daniel Carder · Dessert · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

Rice pudding is one of the most comforting desserts there is, and it uses pantry staples you probably already have.

Serve it warm or chilled, with a sprinkle of cinnamon or a spoonful of jam.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Rice pudding reminds me of the desserts my family made when money was tight but we still wanted something sweet. It's just rice, milk and sugar. I serve it warm in the winter and cold in the Florida summer.– Daniel

Stir often

Milk scorches easily. Stirring frequently over low heat keeps it from sticking and helps the rice release starch for a creamy texture.

Creamy Stovetop Rice Pudding

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook30 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup white rice
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Raisins (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add the rice, milk, sugar and salt to a heavy pot.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
  3. Reduce to low and cook for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  4. Stir in the raisins and cinnamon.
  5. Serve warm or chill for 2 hours.

Pantry swaps

  • Leftover rice? Use 1 1/2 cups cooked rice and 2 cups milk; simmer 15 minutes.
  • Dairy-free? Use coconut or oat milk.

Rusty Chef tip: It thickens a lot as it cools. Take it off the heat while still slightly loose.

Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
