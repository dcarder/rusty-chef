Rusty's Smokestack Flatbread

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 35 minutes

Jump to recipe

This is a Rusty Chef original. Instead of tomato sauce, the flatbreads get a creamy garlic sauce made with the bacon drippings, so the smoky bacon flavor is in every bite.

Sweet corn and a drizzle of warm honey balance the salty bacon. Use store-bought naan, or make the bases with our Skillet Flatbread recipe.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I don't know if this kind of pizza exists or not, but you have to do something different.– Daniel

Put the bacon in the sauce

Most bacon pizzas just scatter bacon on top. Here the drippings left in the pan become the base of a quick garlic and parmesan cream sauce, which is what makes this flatbread taste so smoky and rich.

Rusty's Smokestack Flatbread

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$2.92/serving

Ingredients

  • 8 slices bacon, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 4 large flatbreads or naan, store-bought or homemade
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 3/4 cup canned or frozen corn, drained
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment or foil.
  2. Cook the bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Move it to a paper towel and pour off all but 1 tablespoon of the drippings.
  3. Add the butter to the drippings over medium-low heat. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds, then the flour for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until thick, then stir in the parmesan and salt. Let it cool slightly.
  4. Lay the flatbreads on the baking sheets. Spread the sauce thinly over each one, leaving a small border. Top with the mozzarella, corn, bacon and half the green onions.
  5. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the edges are crisp and the cheese is bubbling. Warm the honey for about 15 seconds in the microwave, drizzle it over the flatbreads, scatter the rest of the green onions on top and cut into strips or wedges.

Pantry swaps

  • Want a sharper bite? Use cheddar for half of the mozzarella.
  • No naan or flatbread? Use pizza dough, pita or large flour tortillas. Tortillas need only 6 to 8 minutes.

Rusty Chef tip: Cook the bacon until it's properly crisp before it goes on. It won't crisp much more in the short bake.

Storage

Best fresh. Leftovers keep for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a dry skillet or a hot oven to crisp the bottom.
