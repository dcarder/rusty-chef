3-Ingredient Peanut Butter Cookies

By Daniel Carder · Dessert · 15 minutes

Jump to recipe

These cookies need just peanut butter, sugar and an egg, and they're naturally flour-free.

They're soft in the middle, slightly crisp at the edges and ready in about 20 minutes.

From Daniel's kitchen

These three-ingredient cookies have saved me more than once when the kids wanted a treat and I didn't want to go to the store. Peanut butter, sugar and an egg are always in my kitchen. The kids love pressing the fork marks on top.

Use regular peanut butter

Natural peanut butter separates and makes cookies oily and crumbly. Standard creamy peanut butter gives the best texture.

3-Ingredient Peanut Butter Cookies

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total15 min
  • Serves18

Ingredients

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet.
  2. Mix the peanut butter, sugar and egg until smooth.
  3. Roll into 1-inch balls and space 2 inches apart.
  4. Press each with a fork in a crisscross pattern.
  5. Bake for 9 to 10 minutes. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before moving.

Pantry swaps

  • Want chocolate? Press a few chocolate chips into each cookie.
  • Brown sugar? Gives a chewier, more caramel-like cookie.

Rusty Chef tip: They'll look underdone when you take them out. They firm up as they cool.

Storage

Keeps 5 days in an airtight container.
