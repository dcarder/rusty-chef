Pasta e Fagioli

By Daniel Carder · Soup · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

Pasta e fagioli means pasta and beans. It's an Italian peasant dish somewhere between a soup and a stew.

Everything comes from the cupboard, and it's filling enough for dinner on its own.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

This is a big, cozy pot of food that costs very little to make. I cook it when I need dinner to stretch into a few lunches, too. The kids like it more than I expected; I think it's the little pasta in every spoonful.– Daniel

Let it rest

Like most bean dishes, this thickens and improves as it sits for 5 minutes off the heat.

Pasta e Fagioli

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) white or kidney beans
  • 4 cups stock
  • 1 cup small pasta
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the onion in oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes, beans, stock and herbs. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Mash a few of the beans to thicken.
  4. Add the pasta and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until tender.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, season and serve with parmesan.

Pantry swaps

  • No small pasta? Broken spaghetti.
  • No beans? Canned chickpeas or lentils.

Rusty Chef tip: Add a pinch of chili flakes with the garlic for gentle warmth.

Storage

Keeps 4 days. Add water when reheating as the pasta absorbs liquid.
