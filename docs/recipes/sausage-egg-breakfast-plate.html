Sausage, Egg and Pepper Breakfast Plate

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 30 minutes

Jump to recipe

This is the big weekend breakfast you'd order at a diner, made in one skillet.

Crispy potatoes, browned sausage and peppers soak up all the flavor before the eggs go on top.

Give the potatoes a head start

Cut the potatoes small and cook them first. Small cubes crisp up in about 10 minutes, and the sausage and peppers only need a few minutes after that.

Sausage, Egg and Pepper Breakfast Plate

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$2.40/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 sausages or 1/2 lb breakfast sausage
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika (optional)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Toast, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the potatoes with a pinch of salt and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until golden and tender.
  2. Push the potatoes to the side. Add the sausage (sliced, or crumbled if loose) and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until browned.
  3. Add the pepper, onion and paprika and cook for 4 minutes until softened. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Make 4 small wells in the mixture and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the whites are set.
  5. Divide between plates and serve with toast.

Pantry swaps

  • No sausage? Use bacon, ham or leave it out and add a can of drained beans.
  • No fresh potatoes? Use frozen hash browns or diced potatoes.

Rusty Chef tip: Dry the potato cubes with a towel before cooking. Less moisture means crispier potatoes.

Storage

The hash keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Cook fresh eggs when you reheat it.
