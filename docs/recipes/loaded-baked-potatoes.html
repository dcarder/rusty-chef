Loaded Baked Potatoes

By Daniel Carder · Dinner · 65 minutes

Jump to recipe

A baked potato is a blank canvas and one of the cheapest filling meals there is.

Load them up with chili, beans, cheese, broccoli, sour cream, or any leftovers from the fridge.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Baked potato night is a hit in our house because everybody builds their own. It's one of the cheapest dinners I make, and setting out toppings ends the picky-eater battles. Leftover chili or beans make a great topping too.– Daniel

Skip the foil

Wrapping potatoes in foil steams them and makes the skin soft. Bake them bare, rubbed with oil and salt, for crisp, flavorful skin.

Loaded Baked Potatoes

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook60 min
  • Total65 min
  • Serves4
  • Est. cost$1.20/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 can (15 oz) beans, warmed
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter (optional)
  • Sour cream or yogurt (optional)
  • Green onions (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Scrub the potatoes, prick them all over with a fork, then rub with oil and salt.
  3. Bake directly on the oven rack for 50 to 60 minutes until a knife slides in easily.
  4. Split open, fluff the insides with a fork and add butter.
  5. Top with beans, cheese, yogurt and green onions.

Pantry swaps

  • Short on time? Microwave for 8 to 10 minutes, then crisp in the oven for 10.
  • No beans? Leftover chili or broccoli and cheese.

Rusty Chef tip: Squeeze the ends of the baked potato toward the middle to push the fluffy insides up.

Storage

Baked potatoes keep 4 days in the fridge.
