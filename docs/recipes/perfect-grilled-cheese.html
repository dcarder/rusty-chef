Perfect Grilled Cheese

By Daniel Carder · Lunch · 10 minutes

Jump to recipe

Grilled cheese is the classic budget lunch, and a few small tricks make it perfect every time.

Pair it with our canned tomato soup for the ultimate comfort meal.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

Grilled cheese is the lunch my wife and kids never say no to, and when the budget is tight, it's two slices of bread and some cheese. The trick I learned after burning plenty of them: keep the heat low and be patient. On rainy Florida afternoons, I pair it with a pot of tomato soup and everybody's happy.– Daniel

Low heat wins

Medium-low heat gives the cheese time to melt before the bread burns. Starting the sandwich in a cold pan helps too, because the bread and cheese warm up together.

Perfect Grilled Cheese

  • Prep2 min
  • Cook8 min
  • Total10 min
  • Serves1
  • Est. cost$0.95/serving

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 2 slices cheese or 1/2 cup shredded
  • 1 tablespoon butter or mayo

Instructions

  1. Spread butter or mayo on one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Place one slice butter-side down in a cold skillet. Top with the cheese and the second slice, butter-side up.
  3. Turn the heat to medium-low.
  4. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and melted.
  5. Slice and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No butter? Mayo spreads easily and browns even better.
  • Want an upgrade? Add a slice of ham or tomato, or a sprinkle of garlic powder on the outside.

Rusty Chef tip: Cover the pan with a lid for the first couple of minutes to help the cheese melt before the bread gets too dark.

Best fresh.
