Easy Huevos Rancheros

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 20 minutes

Jump to recipe

Huevos rancheros is a Mexican ranch-style breakfast, and almost everything in it comes from a can or a jar.

Crisp tortillas, smoky beans and runny eggs make a breakfast that keeps you full until lunch.

Crisp the tortillas first

A quick fry in a little oil turns soft tortillas into a sturdy, crunchy base that won't go soggy under the beans and salsa.

Easy Huevos Rancheros

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook15 min
  • Total20 min
  • Serves2
  • Est. cost$2.17/serving

Ingredients

  • 4 small corn or flour tortillas
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 tablespoons oil, divided
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt, to taste
  • Sour cream, to serve (optional)
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. Warm the beans with the cumin, a pinch of salt and a splash of water in a small pan, mashing about half of them with a fork.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the tortillas one at a time for about 1 minute per side until crisp in spots. Drain on a paper towel.
  3. Add the rest of the oil to the skillet, lower the heat to medium and fry the eggs until the whites are set and the yolks are still runny, 3 to 4 minutes.
  4. Put two tortillas on each plate, spread with the beans and top each with an egg.
  5. Spoon over the salsa, sprinkle with cheese and finish with sour cream and green onions if you have them.

Pantry swaps

  • No black beans? Pinto, kidney or refried beans all work.
  • No salsa? Simmer a can of diced tomatoes with a pinch of chili powder for 5 minutes.

Rusty Chef tip: Cover the pan for the last minute of frying the eggs. The steam sets the tops of the whites without overcooking the yolks.

Storage

Best fresh. Leftover beans keep for 4 days in the fridge and reheat well.
