Drive-Thru Style Breakfast Egg Sandwich

By Daniel Carder · Breakfast · 13 minutes

Jump to recipe

The drive-thru breakfast sandwich is a morning habit that adds up fast.

Make four at home for about the price of one, and freeze extras for busy mornings.

From Daniel's kitchen ♥

I used to grab drive-thru breakfast sandwiches on busy mornings, and it was quietly costing me a lot every month. Now I make a batch on Sunday, wrap them in foil and freeze them. On crazy mornings, they're ready in the microwave in two minutes and cost about a quarter of the drive-thru price.– Daniel

Get the round egg

Cook the eggs inside a greased canning jar ring, or a mug in the microwave, so they come out perfectly round and fit the muffin.

Drive-Thru Style Breakfast Egg Sandwich

  • Prep5 min
  • Cook8 min
  • Total13 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 4 English muffins, split and toasted
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices ham or Canadian bacon
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Toast and lightly butter the English muffins.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Place greased jar rings in the pan, crack an egg into each and break the yolk.
  3. Add 1 tablespoon water to the pan, cover and cook for 3 minutes until set.
  4. Warm the ham in the pan for 1 minute.
  5. Stack egg, ham and cheese on each muffin and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • No jar rings? Cook the eggs as a flat omelet and cut into squares.
  • Sausage version? Use thin cooked sausage patties instead of ham.

Rusty Chef tip: Wrap extras in foil and freeze. Microwave from frozen for 1 to 2 minutes, wrapped in a paper towel.

Storage

Freeze up to 1 month.
