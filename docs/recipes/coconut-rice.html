Fluffy Coconut Rice

By Daniel Carder · Side · 22 minutes

Jump to recipe

Cooking rice in coconut milk instead of water turns a plain side into something special.

Serve it with curry, stir-fries, beans or grilled vegetables.

From Daniel's kitchen

One can of coconut milk turns plain rice into something my kids actually get excited about. It's a little sweet and very creamy. I serve it with curry or just with some beans and veggies when I want to stretch dinner.

Rinse the rice well

Coconut milk adds richness, so rinsing off excess starch keeps the rice fluffy instead of gummy.

Fluffy Coconut Rice

  • Prep2 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total22 min
  • Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups jasmine or long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Add everything to a pot and stir.
  2. Bring to a boil, stirring once or twice.
  3. Cover, reduce to the lowest heat and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Turn off the heat and rest covered for 10 minutes.
  5. Fluff with a fork and serve.

Pantry swaps

  • Light coconut milk? Works fine, the rice will be less rich.
  • No jasmine rice? Any long-grain white rice.

Rusty Chef tip: Shake the can well before opening so the cream and liquid are evenly mixed.

Storage

Keeps 4 days. Reheat with a splash of water.
