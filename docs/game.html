Burger Stack: The Rusty Chef Game

Help our rusty robot chef build burgers while dodging kitchen pests.

How to play

  • Build the burgers: walk across every part of a bun, patty, cheese, lettuce or tomato slice to knock it down a level. Get every piece onto the plates at the bottom to finish the level.
  • Chain drops: a falling piece knocks down the piece below it.
  • Squash pests: drop a piece on the grease blob, rogue fork or angry onion for 500 points.
  • Oil spray: stuns pests in front of you for a few seconds. You get one extra can each level.
  • Controls: use the arrow buttons and OIL SPRAY on your phone, or the arrow keys (or WASD) and the space bar on a keyboard. Press Enter or P to pause.
